Calculation of Power in an AC Circuit

In an AC circuit, power can be calculated using
dierent formulas depending on whether the circuit
is purely resistive, purely reactive, or a combination
of both. In a purely resistive AC circuit, the
impedance is equal to the resistance (R). The power
(P) can be calculated using the formula:
P = V * I * cos(θ)
In a purely reactive AC circuit, the impedance is equal
to the reactance (X). The power (P) can be
calculated using the formula:
P = V * I * sin(θ)

Electrical Power in an AC Circuit
In a DC circuit, the voltages and currents do not vary.

Electrical Power in an AC Circuit

AC circuits contain reactance. This means

In a circuit that has both resistive and reactive
components, the power factor (PF) is
introduced. The power factor represents the

 General Practical Physics II


Electrical Power in an AC Circuit
The apparent power (S) is the product of voltage
and current, while the real power (P) is the actual
power consumed or transferred in the circuit. The
power factor is given by: PF = P / S

The power can then be calculated as:

P = V * I * PF

A circuit’s average power consumption will be the
average of the instantaneous power over one full
revolution with the instantaneous power, p deﬁned
as the multiplication of the instantaneous voltage, v
by the instantaneous current, i.

SQ3R is a systematic and eective reading strategy designed to enhance
comprehension and retention of complex texts. The acronym stands for Survey,
Question, Read, Recite, and Review. In this approach:

Survey: Readers quickly scan the text, noting headings, subheadings, and other
structural elements to get an overview.
Question: They generate thought-provoking questions about the material to
guide their reading.
Read: They actively read the text, seeking answers to their questions and
identifying key information.
Recite: Readers verbally or mentally summarise what they've learned to reinforce
understanding.
Review: Finally, they revisit their notes and the text itself to ensure long-term
retention.
