energy in a parallel plate capacitor
In the setup of a parallel-plate capacitor with plate area A and plate separation d, the
relationship between the voltage (V) and the electric ﬁeld (E) is given as V=Ed.

 ½ε0E2(A⋅d)

signifying the energy per unit volume within the electric ﬁeld. The total energy stored
between the plates can then be calculated by multiplying the energy per unit volume by
the volume between the plates.
