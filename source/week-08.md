A magnetic dipole refers to a fundamental concept in
physics that describes the behavior of a magnetic
ﬁeld produced by a magnetic object. It is often
represented as a small magnet with two opposite
poles, namely the north pole (N) and the south pole
(S), separated by a certain distance. The term
"dipole" signiﬁes the presence of two poles.
 General Practical Physics II




The behavior of a magnetic dipole can be understood
through its interaction with external magnetic ﬁelds.
When placed in an external magnetic ﬁeld, such as that
produced by another magnet or an electromagnetic
wave, a magnetic dipole experiences a torque that
tends to align it with the external ﬁeld. This
phenomenon is known as magnetization.
General Practical Physics II




The two models for a dipole (current loop and
magnetic poles), give the same predictions for the
magnetic ﬁeld far from the source. However, inside
the source region they give dierent predictions. The
magnetic ﬁeld between poles is in the opposite
direction to the magnetic moment (which points from
the negative charge to the positive charge.

According to right-hand rule, the magnetic dipole
moment points through the loop.
General Practical Physics II




Since the magnetic dipole aligns with the magnetic
ﬁeld, it must have higher potential energy when it is
aligned opposite to the magnetic ﬁeld direction and
lower potential energy when it is aligned with the ﬁeld.
The magnitude can be deﬁned as the current in the
loop multiplied by the area of the loop. The unit for
measuring magnetic dipole moment is A*m2 or J/T.
General Practical Physics II




The formula to measure the magnetic dipole moment of
an electron is given by:

M = G (-eL/2m)

Where,

e = charge of electron,

m = mass of electron at rest

L = angular momentum of the electron

G = correlation factor.

Data abstraction

implemented data abstraction.

Functional abstraction

It involves writing a programme in such a way that the implementation of the
methods of an object is not directly exposed to the outside world or to other
programmes.
If other programmes need to use the method of an object, all they need to know
is the method’s name, the format of the input, and the format of the output.

We can implement abstraction by using access speciﬁers such as public and private for
the properties and functions of an object.

The public and private access speciﬁers restrict the access of other programmes to
only some of the properties and functions of an object

    In Summary

This week, we have learned about the significance of libraries and frameworks in modern web development.
Libraries offer pre-written code for specific tasks, while frameworks provide structured architecture for entire
applications. They enhance efficiency, consistency, scalability, and access to thriving communities and
ecosystems. Front-end libraries like React and Vue.js focus on user interfaces, while back-end frameworks
like Express.js and Django streamline server-side development. Choosing the right library or framework
depends on project requirements, preferred programming language, community support, learning curve, and
scalability needs. Libraries and frameworks are the essential building blocks that empower developers to create
powerful, efficient web applications, whether for front-end interactivity or robust back-end infrastructures.
