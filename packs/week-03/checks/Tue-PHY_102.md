**1.** A torch cell reads 1.50 V on a voltmeter with nothing else connected, and 1.20 V when it is lighting a bulb. What has become of the missing 0.30 V?
a) The cell's emf has genuinely dropped to 1.20 V because chemical energy is being consumed
b) The voltmeter loads the circuit and under-reads by 0.30 V once current flows
c) It is the potential drop Ir across the cell's own internal resistance, dissipated inside the cell
d) It is stored in the magnetic field of the current-carrying wires and returns when the switch opens

**2.** Why is it wrong to treat electromotive force as a kind of force acting on charges?
a) Because emf is energy supplied per unit charge, measured in joules per coulomb, not in newtons
b) Because emf acts only on the ions inside the cell, whereas force acts on the electrons in the wire
c) Because emf is a rate of energy transfer, so it is a power and is measured in watts
d) Because emf is defined only for alternating sources, while force is defined for steady ones

**3.** In Faraday's law written as emf = -N dPhi/dt, what does the minus sign assert?
a) That the induced emf is always smaller than the applied emf that produced the flux
b) That the induced emf is negative energy, so the coil absorbs rather than supplies power
c) That flux must be decreasing for an emf to be induced at all
d) That the induced emf drives a current whose own flux opposes the change that created it

**4.** A conducting rod slides on frictionless rails through a field B perpendicular to the plane. Which statement describes what actually separates charge in the rod?
a) The changing flux creates a static charge imbalance directly, without any force acting on carriers
b) Each free charge q moves with the rod at speed v, so it feels a magnetic force qvB along the rod that pushes carriers towards one end
c) Friction between the rod and the rails strips electrons and deposits them at one end
d) The magnetic field magnetises the metal, and the resulting poles attract electrons to one end

**5.** The expression one-half epsilon-nought E squared is said to be the energy stored per unit volume in an electric field. What is the physical claim being made?
a) That the energy is located in the field itself, so a region of empty space carrying a field holds energy
b) That energy resides only on the surfaces of the plates, and the expression is a bookkeeping convenience
c) That the energy belongs to the dielectric molecules, so the expression is zero in a vacuum
d) That the energy is carried by the moving electrons in the connecting wires while the capacitor charges

**6.** Why does the energy stored in a capacitor come out as one-half QV rather than QV?
a) Because half the charge always leaks back through the dielectric while the capacitor is charging
b) Because only half of the plate area carries charge at any instant
c) Because the field between the plates occupies only half the volume Ad
d) Because the p.d. rises from zero to V as charge is added, so each element of charge is moved through an average p.d. of V/2

**7.** Faraday found that filling the gap between capacitor plates with an insulator multiplies the capacitance by a factor K. What does a larger K mean physically for the capacitor?
a) The insulator conducts a little, so charge leaks across and the plates hold more of it
b) The dielectric partly cancels the field of the plate charge, so the same charge produces a smaller p.d. and Q/V is larger
c) The dielectric increases the plate separation d, which raises C because C is proportional to d
d) The dielectric raises the emf of the source that charges it, so more charge is driven onto the plates

**8.** Two identical capacitors are to store the largest possible energy from one supply of fixed potential difference V. Why does the parallel arrangement win?
a) Because in parallel each capacitor receives only half the voltage, and halving V doubles the stored energy
b) Because in series the same charge passes through both, and shared charge always means shared energy
c) Because the equivalent capacitance in parallel is the sum, which is larger than any series combination, and at fixed V the energy CV^2/2 grows with C
d) Because in parallel the plate separation is effectively halved, which raises the field between the plates

**9. (explain why)** Explain why the graph of terminal p.d. V against current I for a real cell is a straight line with a negative gradient, and why the intercept on the V axis is the emf.

**10. (explain why)** Explain why a rod sliding along the direction of the magnetic field lines produces no motional emf, while the same rod sliding perpendicular to them does.

**11. (explain why)** Explain why the energy of a charged capacitor can legitimately be said to reside in the empty space between the plates rather than on the plates themselves.
