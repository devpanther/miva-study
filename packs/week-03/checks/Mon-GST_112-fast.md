# Monday — GST_112 fast-hour check

*Portuguese incursion, the trans-Atlantic slave trade, abolition, Lagos and company rule.*
*Five questions, straight after the fast hour. Recall only. Score out of 5.*

**1.** Which European power first ran a regular slave trade from West Africa to the Americas?
A. The Dutch
B. The British
C. The Portuguese
D. The Spanish

**2.** Who is cited for the figure of over 7 million Africans dying in transit?
A. Phillip Curtin
B. Paul Lovejoy
C. Wole Soyinka
D. Claude Ake

**3.** Who sponsored the 1833 bill to abolish slavery?
A. Thomas Fowell Buxton
B. Macgregor Laird
C. John Beecroft
D. George Goldie-Taubman

**4.** After the bombardment of Lagos, who fled to Epe?
A. Akitoye
B. Beecroft
C. Consul Hewett
D. Kosoko

**5.** In which year was the Royal Charter finally granted, creating the Royal Niger Company?
A. 1881
B. 1882
C. 1884
D. 1886

## Answers

**1. C** — The Portuguese were first and developed the triangular route; the Dutch joined in the 17th century, the British were merely the biggest in the 18th, and Spain held the Americas under the Pope's division.

**2. B** — Lovejoy gives the deaths in transit; Curtin called it the largest intercontinental migration over 300 years, Soyinka named the twin evils of slavery and colonialism, and Ake blamed stagnant productive forces.

**3. A** — Buxton, a British parliamentarian, argued the trade must be stopped from Africa itself. Laird ran the 1857 expedition, Beecroft was appointed in 1849, and Goldie-Taubman amalgamated the trading companies.

**4. D** — Kosoko refused the 1851 treaty, was bombarded, and fled to Epe; Akitoye regained the seat on 29 December 1851. Beecroft and Hewett were British officials, not claimants.

**5. D** — 1881 the application was refused for lack of capacity, by 1882 capital reached one million pounds but French firms blocked it, and 1886 the charter was granted. 1884 is the Hewett tour and the Oil River Protectorate.
