## Short answer

**13.** A cell of emf 12 V and internal resistance 0.60 Ω delivers a current of 4.0 A. Calculate the terminal potential difference and the power dissipated inside the cell, and state the external resistance.

**14.** Starting from the Lorentz force on a free charge in a rod, show that the emf induced in a rod of length l moving with speed v perpendicular to a uniform field B is ε = Bvl.

**15.** A parallel-plate capacitor in vacuum has A = 0.040 m² and d = 0.0020 m, with a field of 500 N/C between the plates. Find the capacitance, the potential difference, and the total energy stored, working the energy out from the energy density and then checking it with ½CV².

## Answers

**1. c** — I = ε/(R + r) = 6.0/6.0 = 1.0 A, so V = ε − Ir = 6.0 − 1.0 = 5.0 V.
**2. a** — the three characteristics named for a conductor are free electron movement, zero internal electric field in electrostatic equilibrium, and low resistance.
**3. d** — ε = Bvl = 0.50 × 4.0 × 0.25 = 0.50 V.
**4. b** — V = ε − rI, a straight line of gradient −r with intercept ε.
**5. a** — with I = 0 the lost volts Ir vanish, so V = ε.
**6. c** — u = ½ε₀E² = ½ × 8.85 × 10⁻¹² × 10⁶ = 4.43 × 10⁻⁶ J/m³.
**7. b** — U = ½CV² = ½ × 2.0 × 10⁻⁶ × 10⁴ = 1.0 × 10⁻² J.
**8. d** — Q is fixed once the battery is removed, so V = Q/C falls by K and E = V/d falls with it.
**9. a** — |ε| = N ΔΦ/Δt = 50 × 4.0 × 10⁻³/0.020 = 10 V.
**10. c** — parallel adds capacitances, and at fixed V the energy ½CV² rises with C.
**11. d** — insulators lack free charge carriers, and their high resistivity is what resists the flow of electricity, heat and sound.
**12. b** — emf and potential are energy per unit charge, J/C.
**13.** V = ε − Ir = 12 − 4.0 × 0.60 = 9.6 V; power inside = I²r = 16 × 0.60 = 9.6 W; R = V/I = 9.6/4.0 = 2.4 Ω.
**14.** A charge q in the rod moves with the rod at speed v, so it feels F = qvB along the rod. Moving it the length l does work W = FL = qvBl. Emf is work per unit charge, so ε = W/q = Bvl. (Equivalently Φ = Blx and ε = dΦ/dt = Blv.)
**15.** C = ε₀A/d = 8.85 × 10⁻¹² × 0.040/0.0020 = 1.77 × 10⁻¹⁰ F; V = Ed = 500 × 0.0020 = 1.0 V; u = ½ε₀E² = ½ × 8.85 × 10⁻¹² × 2.5 × 10⁵ = 1.106 × 10⁻⁶ J/m³; volume = Ad = 8.0 × 10⁻⁵ m³, so U = 8.85 × 10⁻¹¹ J. Check: ½CV² = ½ × 1.77 × 10⁻¹⁰ × 1.0 = 8.85 × 10⁻¹¹ J.
