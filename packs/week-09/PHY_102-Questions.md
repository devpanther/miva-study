## Section B — Short answer (3)

**13.** State the Lorentz force law in full, defining every symbol and giving its SI unit. Then explain precisely: (i) the **two distinct** circumstances in which the magnetic term vanishes while the electric term does not; (ii) why the magnetic force does **no work** whereas the electric force does, and what physical quantity is therefore conserved; (iii) how right-hand rule 1 gives the direction of the magnetic force, and exactly what changes when the charge is negative.

**14.** Derive **F = I L × B** for a straight wire from the single-charge law **F = qv × B**, using dq = λ dl and I = λv. Then show that a charge entering a uniform field with velocity components both along and across **B** follows a **helix**, deriving **r = mv⊥/(qB)** and **T = 2πm/(qB)**, and explain why the period does not depend on the speed while the radius does.

**15.** (i) A 15 cm length of wire carries 6.0 A at 30° to a uniform 0.25 T field; find the force on it and state its direction relative to the wire and the field. (ii) A proton enters the same 0.30 T field at 2.0×10⁶ m/s perpendicular to it; find the radius and period of its orbit and its kinetic energy after ten revolutions, given that its kinetic energy on entry was 3.3×10⁻¹⁵ J. (iii) What electric field, and in what orientation, would make that proton travel straight through? (iv) An X-ray has wavelength 0.10 nm; find its frequency and its energy per photon in joules and in electronvolts.

## Answers

**1. D** — *The Lorentz force is a vector sum, not an arithmetic one.* F = qE + qv × B, and the resultant of a 12 N and a 5 N force depends entirely on the angle between them: it is 17 N if they are parallel, 7 N if antiparallel, 13 N if perpendicular, and any value in between otherwise. Nothing in the question fixes that angle. A is the trap the deck's own mid-lesson answer falls into — adding magnitudes, which is right only for parallel forces. B assumes the two are always perpendicular; qv × B is always ⊥ to **B**, but **E** need not be along **B**, so F_E and F_B are not generally at 90°. C assumes they always oppose. The examinable point is that the geometry must be given before any of the three can be chosen.

**2. C** — *F_B ⊥ v ⇒ P = F·v = 0.* Work is ∫F·dl, and dl is along v, so a force permanently perpendicular to v contributes nothing at any instant: the speed, and hence the kinetic energy, cannot change. A is a vague energy-bookkeeping story with no mechanism, and would equally forbid the (real) work done by electric fields. B invokes induction, which requires a *changing* flux and is irrelevant to a static field — and if a cancellation were the reason, the result would fail whenever no induced field existed. D makes the result quantitative and therefore defeasible: a strong enough field would then do work. The truth is exact and geometric, not a matter of size.

**3. B** — *F = qvB sin θ with θ = 0 gives zero force.* v ∥ B means v × B = 0: no magnetic force at all, so the proton sails straight on at constant velocity. This is why charged particles in space spiral **along** field lines rather than across them. A applies r = mv/qB without checking that the formula assumes v ⊥ B. C is the sin/cos inversion — the force is greatest when v ⊥ B and zero when they are aligned. D imagines a retarding force; the magnetic force never changes speed even when it is non-zero.

**4. B** — *r = mv/(qB), and the sign of q sets the sense.* |q| is the same for both, but m_e is about 1/1836 of m_p, so the electron's radius is smaller by that factor. Its charge is negative, so **F = qv × B** points the opposite way and it circles the opposite way round. A gets the size right and the sense wrong — the commonest half-answer, from remembering the formula but not that q is signed. C confuses the *period's* independence of speed with a supposed independence of mass; the radius is proportional to m. D inverts the mass dependence, as though a light particle were harder to bend.

**5. B** — *v = E/B, the velocity-selector condition.* Undeflected means qE = qvB, so v = E/B = (6.0×10⁴)/(0.15) = **4.0×10⁵ m/s**. A multiplies instead of dividing. C inverts, giving B/E. D is the misconception the device is built to defeat: q cancels from both sides and m never enters, so the selected speed is the same for every particle, positive or negative, light or heavy. (Mass only enters at the *next* stage, where B alone bends the beam to r = mv/qB.)

**6. B** — *F = BIL sin θ, θ between the current and B, L in metres.* F = (0.50)(4.0)(0.60)(sin 25°) = (1.2)(0.4226) = **0.51 N**. A is 1.1 N = BIL cos 25°, the sin/cos swap. C is 1.2 N = BIL, the angle ignored altogether — the value only reached when the wire is perpendicular to the field. D is 51 N, from leaving L as 60 cm: a factor of 100, and a force that would tear the wire out of its mounting.

**7. B** — *F = qv × B: up × north = west.* Taking east = x̂, north = ŷ, up = ẑ, we need ẑ × ŷ = −x̂, i.e. **due west**. By RHR-1: thumb up (v), fingers north (B), palm faces west, and the charge is positive so that is the force. A is the same calculation with the hands or the cross-product order reversed — the single commonest direction error, and it is a 180° error, not a small one. C puts F along v, which a cross product can never do. D puts F along B, likewise impossible: F ⊥ both.

**8. B** — *Constant speed, changing direction, therefore changing momentum.* Zero work fixes |v| and hence ½mv², but **p = mv is a vector**: its direction turns continuously round the circle, so p is not constant, and indeed reverses completely each half revolution. A conflates a scalar invariant with a vector one. C is the opposite error — no work does not mean no force; there is a real centripetal force qvB and a real acceleration qvB/m, both perpendicular to v. D says the acceleration vanishes, which would leave the particle undeflected and make the entire topic empty.

**9. C** — *T = 2πm/(qB) contains no v.* Doubling the speed doubles the radius, r = mv/qB, so the particle travels twice as far round a circle twice as big at twice the speed: the lap time is identical. This isochronism is what makes the cyclotron possible — the accelerating voltage can be reversed at one fixed frequency for the whole spiral. A and B both assume the radius is fixed and only the speed changes, which is precisely what does not happen. D squares the (non-existent) dependence.

**10. A** — *From B = F/(qv).* B has units N/(C·m s⁻¹) = **N·s/(C·m)**, equivalently N/(A·m) since 1 A = 1 C/s. This is exactly the deck's "the Tesla equals a Newton/(coulomb × meter/sec)". B omits the seconds, i.e. treats the velocity as dimensionless. C is N·m/A, which is a weber per metre — dimensionally a magnetic *flux* per length, not a flux density. D is the reciprocal of the correct combination, the result of solving B = F/qv upside down.

**11. C** — *An EM wave is transverse: E ⊥ B ⊥ direction of travel.* With propagation along +x and E along ±y, B must lie along **±z**, the sense being that in which **E × B** points along the direction of travel. A is the standard wrong answer, importing the picture of a longitudinal (sound) wave; an EM wave has **no** component of either field along the direction of travel. B makes E and B parallel, which would leave no third direction for propagation and contradicts the induction mechanism, in which each field's *change* generates the other at right angles. D denies the wave a polarisation, when in fact the two fields are rigidly locked in phase and in orientation.

**12. A** — *λ = c/f.* λ = (3.00×10⁸)/(2.45×10⁹) = 0.1224 m = **12.2 cm** — a wavelength comparable with the oven cavity, which is exactly why the standing-wave hot spots exist and why the turntable is needed. B is out by a factor of ten, from mishandling the 10⁹ in "giga". C is c × f: multiplying when the relation c = fλ requires dividing. D is f/c = 8.17 m⁻¹, which is the *wavenumber* (per metre) quoted as though it were a length; the units alone rule it out.

**13.** *Concept: the Lorentz force law, the conditions for each term, the no-work property, and RHR-1.* **The law:** **F = qE + q(v × B) = q(E + v × B)**, where **F** is the total electromagnetic force in newtons (N), **q** is the charge in coulombs (C) **including its sign**, **E** is the electric field in newtons per coulomb (N/C, identical to V/m), **v** is the particle's velocity in metres per second (m/s), and **B** is the magnetic flux density in tesla (T = N·s/(C·m) = N/(A·m)). The magnitude of the magnetic term is **F = qvB sin θ**, θ being the angle between **v** and **B**. **(i) Two ways the magnetic term dies.** First, if the particle is **at rest** (v = 0): a stationary charge feels no magnetic force whatever the field, whereas qE acts on it regardless. Second, if **v is parallel or antiparallel to B** (θ = 0° or 180°), because sin θ = 0 and a cross product of parallel vectors vanishes; the particle then moves straight along the field line at constant speed, which is why charged particles follow field lines rather than cross them. In both cases the electric term qE is unaffected, since it involves neither v nor any angle. **(ii) Work.** The magnetic force is, by the geometry of the cross product, **perpendicular to v at every instant**, so its instantaneous power is **P = F·v = 0** and the work over any path is zero. It follows that **speed and kinetic energy are conserved**; only the *direction* of the momentum changes. The electric force qE has no such constraint — it has a component along v in general, so it changes speed and does work, which is how particles are actually accelerated. The correct division of labour in every accelerator is: **E accelerates, B steers**. **(iii) Direction.** RHR-1 as the deck states it: point the **thumb along v**, the **fingers along B**, and the force emerges **perpendicular to the palm**. This gives the force on a **positive** charge. For a **negative** charge — an electron — the force is in exactly the **opposite** direction, because q multiplies the whole vector; the safe procedure is to work out the positive-charge answer with the right hand and then reverse it. In all cases **F is perpendicular to the plane containing v and B**, so it is never along v and never along B.

**14.** *Concept: from qv × B to I L × B, and the helical orbit.* **The wire.** Consider a wire carrying free charge of linear density **λ** (coulombs per metre) drifting with velocity **v**. An element of length **dl** contains charge **dq = λ dl**. The magnetic force on it is the single-charge law applied to that packet: **dF = (dq) v × B = (λ dl) v × B**. Since λ and dl are scalars and v is the vector, this is **dF = (λv) dl × B**, and the current is **I = λv** — charge per unit length times speed is charge per unit time. Hence **dF = I dl × B**, and for a straight wire of length L in a **uniform** field, integrating gives **F = I L × B**, magnitude **F = BIL sin θ** with θ between the current and the field. The deck's remark that the vector-current and scalar-current forms "are completely the same" is exactly this: the direction can be carried either by I or by dl. Note the force is **zero** when the wire lies along B and **maximum, BIL**, when it is perpendicular. **The helix.** Resolve the entry velocity into **v∥** (along B) and **v⊥** (perpendicular to B). Because **F = qv × B** is perpendicular to **B**, the force has **no component along B**, so **v∥ is never changed**: that part of the motion is uniform and straight. The perpendicular part experiences a force of constant magnitude **qv⊥B**, always perpendicular to **v⊥** and hence always centre-seeking, which is precisely uniform circular motion. Setting the magnetic force equal to the centripetal requirement, **qv⊥B = mv⊥²/r**, gives **r = mv⊥/(qB)**. The circumference is 2πr, traversed at v⊥, so **T = 2πr/v⊥ = 2πm/(qB)**. Superposing the uniform drift along B on the circle gives a **helix** of radius r and **pitch v∥T**. **Why T is speed-independent but r is not.** In r = mv⊥/qB the speed appears explicitly: a faster particle is harder to bend and sweeps a bigger circle, r ∝ v⊥. But the period is the circumference divided by the speed, and the circumference is itself proportional to the speed — the two factors of v⊥ cancel exactly. A faster particle has further to go and goes proportionately faster, so it arrives at the same time. This **isochronism** is why a cyclotron can be driven at a single fixed frequency **f = qB/2πm** while the particles spiral outward gaining energy.

**15.** *Concept: F = BIL sin θ, r = mv/qB and T = 2πm/qB, the crossed-field condition, and c = fλ with E = hf.* **(i)** L = 15 cm = 0.15 m. **F = BIL sin θ = (0.25)(6.0)(0.15)(sin 30°) = (0.225)(0.500) = 0.11 N** (1.1×10⁻¹ N). Its direction is **perpendicular to the plane containing the wire and B** — sideways to the wire, and also perpendicular to the field; by RHR-1 with the thumb along the conventional current. It is never along the wire and never along B. **(ii)** **r = mv/(qB) = (1.67×10⁻²⁷)(2.0×10⁶)/[(1.60×10⁻¹⁹)(0.30)] = (3.34×10⁻²¹)/(4.80×10⁻²⁰) = 7.0×10⁻² m = 7.0 cm.** **T = 2πm/(qB) = 2π(1.67×10⁻²⁷)/(4.80×10⁻²⁰) = 2.2×10⁻⁷ s.** After ten revolutions the kinetic energy is still **3.3×10⁻¹⁵ J** — unchanged, because the magnetic force is perpendicular to v and does **no work**; ten revolutions, or ten million, make no difference. (Check: ½mv² = ½(1.67×10⁻²⁷)(2.0×10⁶)² = 3.3×10⁻¹⁵ J ✔, so the stated entry energy is consistent.) **(iii)** For straight-line motion the electric force must cancel the magnetic one: **E = vB = (2.0×10⁶)(0.30) = 6.0×10⁵ V/m**. It must be **perpendicular to both v and B** — i.e. along the line of the magnetic force — and directed so that **qE opposes qv × B**; for the proton that means E points **opposite** to the magnetic force it would otherwise feel. The three vectors v, B and E are then mutually perpendicular: a velocity selector. **(iv)** λ = 0.10 nm = 1.0×10⁻¹⁰ m, so **f = c/λ = (3.00×10⁸)/(1.0×10⁻¹⁰) = 3.0×10¹⁸ Hz**. **E = hf = (6.63×10⁻³⁴)(3.0×10¹⁸) = 2.0×10⁻¹⁵ J**, and in electronvolts E = (1.99×10⁻¹⁵)/(1.60×10⁻¹⁹) = **1.2×10⁴ eV ≈ 12 keV** — several thousand times the few-eV energy of a visible photon, which is why X-rays ionise tissue and visible light does not.
