# Monday — GST_112 fast-hour check

*The named reorientation strategies: NOA, OFN, MAMSER, WAI and austerity measures.*
*Five questions, straight after the fast hour. Recall only. Score out of 5.*

**1.** Which decree established the National Orientation Agency?
A. Decree 100 of 1993
B. Decree 100 of 1987
C. Decree 19 of 1976
D. Decree 87 of 1990

**2.** Operation Feed the Nation was introduced by whose military government?
A. General Yakubu Gowon
B. General Olusegun Obasanjo
C. General Muhammadu Buhari
D. General Ibrahim Babangida

**3.** MAMSER was launched on the recommendation of a Public Bureau headed by whom?
A. Moses Ochonu
B. Abimbola Oyarinu
C. Samuel Cookey
D. J. B. Denen

**4.** War Against Indiscipline ended in which month and year?
A. January 1970
B. December 1983
C. March 1986
D. September 1985

**5.** Under which programme does the deck say austerity was further developed?
A. Buhari's War Against Indiscipline
B. Babangida's Structural Adjustment Programme
C. Obasanjo's Operation Feed the Nation
D. Gowon's 3 R's policy

## Answers

**1. A** — The deck dates the NOA to Decree 100 of 1993. 1987 is MAMSER's launch year and 1976 belongs to Operation Feed the Nation, so those decree numbers pair the right year with the wrong body.

**2. B** — OFN (1976-80) was introduced by Obasanjo's military government. Gowon ran the 3 R's, Buhari ran War Against Indiscipline, and Babangida ran SAP.

**3. C** — The deck names Samuel Cookey as head of the Public Bureau whose recommendation produced MAMSER in 1987. The other three are scholars cited in the course, not officials.

**4. D** — The deck ends WAI in September 1985, with Buhari's military regime. January 1970 is Biafra's surrender and 1986 is DFRRI's year.

**5. B** — The deck's mid-lesson answer is Babangida and SAP, even though its narrative slide credits Buhari's regime with introducing austerity after the 1970s oil boom. Answer as the deck answers.
