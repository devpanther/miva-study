# Monday — GST_112 fast-hour check

*The named reorientation strategies: NOA, OFN, MAMSER, WAI and austerity measures.*
*5 questions, straight after the hour. Score out of 5.*

**1.** Under a military government, a brigade punishes people for jumping queues, arriving late and dropping litter, and is later criticised for high-handedness. Which reorientation strategy is this?
A. National Orientation Agency (NOA)
B. Operation Feed the Nation (OFN)
C. War Against Indiscipline (WAI)
D. MAMSER

**2.** Put these reorientation strategies in the order they were launched: (A) National Orientation Agency; (B) MAMSER; (C) Operation Feed the Nation; (D) War Against Indiscipline.
A. C, B, D, A
B. C, D, B, A
C. D, C, B, A
D. C, D, A, B

**3.** Which of the following statements about austerity measures in Nigeria is correct?
A. Austerity was introduced after the oil boom of the 1970s, during Buhari's military regime
B. Austerity means expanding government borrowing to stimulate demand
C. The austerity measures begun in 2015 were a response to high oil prices
D. Austerity has succeeded because government waste was cut first

**4.** The 1987 Directorate for Social Mobilisation, MAMSER, was phased out and its functions absorbed by a successor body. Which body, and by what instrument was it created?
A. Operation Feed the Nation, by Decree 100 of 1993
B. The WAI Brigade, by Decree 100 of 1985
C. The National Orientation Agency, by the Public Bureau headed by Samuel Cookey
D. The National Orientation Agency, by Decree 100 of 1993

**5.** What are reorientation strategies?
A. Government authorities, institutions or programmes aimed at reshaping people's attitudes, beliefs or behaviours towards a set of ideals
B. Strict, frugal economic policies used by the government to manage public debt
C. The duties every citizen must fulfil under Section 24 of the Constitution
D. Agricultural initiatives aimed at reducing dependence on food imports

---

## Answers

**1. C** — *Identifying a reorientation strategy from its methods.* The War Against Indiscipline, organised by Buhari's military dictatorship, promoted punctuality, respect for queues, environmental cleanliness and adherence to traffic regulations, enforced by the WAI Brigade, whose high-handedness was its utmost criticism. OFN was an agricultural programme, MAMSER a 1987 mobilisation for self-reliance and economic recovery, and the NOA a 1993 agency for public awareness of government policies.

**2. B** — *Chronology of reorientation strategies.* Operation Feed the Nation ran from 1976 to 1980 under Obasanjo's military government (C); the War Against Indiscipline was Buhari's programme and ended in September 1985 (D); MAMSER was launched in 1987 on the Cookey Bureau's recommendation (B); the NOA, MAMSER's successor, was established by Decree 100 of 1993 (A). The other orders put MAMSER before WAI, WAI before OFN, or the NOA before the body it replaced.

**3. A** — *Austerity measures in Nigeria.* Austerity measures were introduced after the oil boom of the 1970s during Buhari's military regime, further developed under Babangida's SAP, and revived from 2015. Austerity is the opposite of stimulus borrowing: strict, frugal policies to manage public debt. The 2015 measures were to plug a budget deficit arising from low oil prices and low output. Austerity has continually failed precisely because waste in government was not addressed and the burden was shifted onto the masses.

**4. D** — *MAMSER and its successor, the NOA.* The National Orientation Agency is the successor of the Directorate for Social Mobilisation (MAMSER) and was established by Decree 100 of 1993. The Public Bureau headed by Samuel Cookey recommended MAMSER's launch in 1987; it did not create the NOA. OFN and the WAI Brigade were earlier programmes, and 1985 is the year WAI ended, not the year of any decree creating a successor.

**5. A** — *Definition of reorientation strategies.* Reorientation strategies are government authorities, institutions or programmes aimed at reshaping or redirecting people's attitudes, beliefs or behaviours towards a particular purpose or set of ideals, using public awareness campaigns, educational programmes, skill development, counselling and community outreach. Strict, frugal economic policies define austerity measures, which are only one such strategy; Section 24 duties are civic responsibilities; food-import reduction was the aim of Operation Feed the Nation alone.
