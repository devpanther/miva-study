# Tuesday — PHY_102 nightly check

*The Lorentz force as concepts: what each term in F = q(E + v × B) means and when each vanishes, why the magnetic force is perpendicular to both v and B and therefore does no work, the RHR-1 direction rule and what the sign of q changes, why the orbit is a circle or a helix and why its period is speed-independent, how the crossed-field selector works, why F = I L × B is the single-charge law summed over a wire, and the transverse geometry of an electromagnetic wave.*
*Sit cold, notes closed, about 15 minutes. Score out of 12.*

**1.** The magnetic part of the Lorentz force on a charged particle is found to be **zero**, although the particle is inside a strong uniform magnetic field. The possible reasons are:
a) the field is uniform, and a uniform field exerts no force on a point charge — only a torque on a dipole
b) the particle is at rest, or its velocity lies along B; in either case v × B = 0
c) the particle's charge is negative, so the minus sign cancels the force
d) the particle's velocity is perpendicular to B, which is where the sine factor passes through zero

**2.** A charged particle moves through a uniform magnetic field in such a way that the magnetic force on it is a **maximum**. It follows that:
a) its velocity is parallel to B, the two vectors reinforcing each other
b) its velocity is perpendicular to B, and the force is perpendicular to both v and B
c) its velocity is perpendicular to B, and the force therefore lies along B
d) its velocity is at 45° to B, the angle that balances the sine and cosine factors

**3.** A magnetic field can steer a charged particle but can never increase its kinetic energy. The reason is that:
a) the magnetic force is always perpendicular to the velocity, so its power F·v is zero at every instant
b) the magnetic force is far weaker than the electric force at ordinary speeds, so the work it does is negligible
c) magnetic field lines are closed loops, so any energy gained on one part of the path is given back on another
d) the magnetic force acts on the particle's spin rather than on its translational motion

**4.** A charge enters a uniform magnetic field with velocity components both **along** and **across** B, and follows a helix. The helix arises because:
a) the field exerts a force along B that stretches the circular orbit out into a spiral
b) the component of v along B is never changed, since the force has no component along B, while the perpendicular component is turned in a circle
c) the field weakens along its own direction, so the radius grows from turn to turn
d) the particle radiates energy as it circulates, so the closed circle gradually opens out

**5.** The period T = 2πm/(qB) of a charged particle's circular orbit does not depend on its speed. This is because:
a) the magnetic force does no work, so no property of the motion can change
b) a faster particle sweeps a proportionally larger circle, and the extra path length exactly offsets the extra speed
c) the period is fixed by the field alone, and the field has been specified as uniform
d) the speed cancels because it enters the centripetal expression squared and the magnetic force once

**6.** In a velocity selector, one and the same speed passes undeflected for **every** particle admitted — electrons, protons, heavy ions alike. This is because:
a) both forces are proportional to q, so q cancels, and neither force contains the mass at all
b) both the electric and the magnetic force are proportional to v, so their ratio is fixed whatever the particle
c) heavier particles are deflected less, and that compensates exactly for their greater inertia
d) the field strengths are re-adjusted for each species before its beam is admitted

**7.** The force on a current-carrying wire, F = I L × B, is:
a) an independent law, obtained from Ampère's law rather than from the Lorentz force
b) the single-charge law qv × B summed over the moving charges: dq = λ dl and I = λv give dF = I dl × B
c) valid only for wires, the Lorentz force being valid only for isolated charges, so the two are logically independent
d) a consequence of Newton's third law applied between the field and the wire

**8.** The deck writes the force on a current element as **dF = (I/c) dl × B**. Judged as an SI formula, this expression:
a) is correct, the c being the conversion from amperes to the units in which B is measured
b) is correct only for relativistic currents, in which the drift speed approaches c
c) is the cgs (Gaussian) form; in SI the factor 1/c is absent and the law reads dF = I dl × B
d) is dimensionally sound in both systems, so either version may be used with SI data

**9. (explain why)** Explain why the magnetic force does no work, and say exactly what is conserved as a result and what is not. Then explain why "does no work" is **not** the same as "exerts no force" and **not** the same as "produces no acceleration". Finish by explaining how a cyclotron nevertheless raises a proton's energy to many MeV.

**10. (explain why)** From the two defining properties of the cross product, explain why the magnetic force is zero when **v** is parallel to **B** and greatest when **v** is perpendicular to **B**, and why the force is perpendicular to both. Then explain what reversing the **sign of the charge** does, and reconcile these two facts: an electron and a proton fired in the **same** direction through the same field curve **opposite** ways, yet an electron beam and a positive-ion beam travelling in **opposite** directions curve the **same** way.

**11. (explain why)** Explain the mechanism of the crossed-field velocity selector: why exactly one speed passes undeflected, why that speed is **E/B** and contains neither the charge nor the mass, what happens to particles faster and slower than the selected speed and **which** force wins in each case, and how adding a second, magnet-only stage converts the apparatus into a mass spectrometer.

**12. (explain why)** For a plane electromagnetic wave, explain why **E** and **B** are perpendicular to each other **and** to the direction of propagation, and what sustains the wave as it travels. Explain why the frequency and the wavelength are inversely related while the speed is fixed at c, and state what physically distinguishes a gamma ray from a radio wave given that both travel at exactly the same speed.

## Answers

**1. b** — *The magnetic force needs motion, and motion across the field.* F = qvB sin θ vanishes in exactly two ways: **v = 0**, so a stationary charge feels nothing magnetic however strong the field; and **sin θ = 0**, i.e. **v parallel or antiparallel to B**, since the cross product of parallel vectors is zero. That is why charged particles spiral **along** field lines rather than across them. (a) imports Week 8's dipole result — a uniform field exerts no net *force on a dipole* — and misapplies it to a single moving charge, which a uniform field pushes very hard indeed. (c) confuses a sign with a magnitude: a negative charge feels a force of the same size in the **opposite** direction, never no force. (d) is the sin/cos inversion: v ⊥ B is where the force is **maximum**, and it is the answer of someone who has memorised "perpendicular" without knowing which quantity it attaches to.

**2. b** — *Maximum force at θ = 90°, and F is still ⊥ to both.* sin θ peaks at 90°, so the force is greatest when v ⊥ B, and F = qv × B is then perpendicular to the plane containing them — perpendicular to v (hence no work) and perpendicular to B. (a) has the angle exactly backwards; parallel vectors give **zero** force. (c) is the sharpest distractor: it gets the angle right and then puts the force **along B**, which a cross product can never do — v × B is perpendicular to B by construction. Anyone who picks (c) knows the magnitude rule and not the direction rule. (d) invents a compromise angle by analogy with projectile range; there is no cosine factor in F = qvB sin θ to balance.

**3. a** — *F ⊥ v ⇒ P = F·v = 0, exactly and at every instant.* Work is ∫F·dl with dl along v, so a force permanently at right angles to the motion contributes nothing anywhere on the path; speed and kinetic energy are therefore rigorously constant. (b) makes the result a matter of size, which would mean a strong enough field could do work — it cannot, at any strength. (c) is the most dangerous answer, because it is nearly a real argument: it concedes that work **is** done and merely claims it cancels round a loop. That would still permit the particle to speed up and slow down within one orbit, which does not happen; and it would fail entirely for a particle on an unclosed helical path. (d) invents a spin interaction; the force qv × B acts on the charge's **translational** velocity, and the result holds for a spinless particle.

**4. b** — *B is untouched by its own cross product, so v∥ survives intact.* Since F = qv × B is perpendicular to **B**, the force has **no component along B**: the parallel part of the velocity is never accelerated or retarded, and drifts on uniformly. The perpendicular part feels a constant-magnitude, always-transverse force and is bent into a circle. Uniform drift plus circle equals **helix**, of radius mv⊥/qB and pitch v∥T. (a) asserts a force along B, which the cross product forbids. (c) invents a non-uniform field; the question specifies a uniform one, and in a genuinely converging field the radius would *shrink*, giving a magnetic mirror, not a uniform helix. (d) appeals to radiation, which is a real but utterly negligible effect here and is not what makes the path helical — the helix appears even in the idealised non-radiating treatment.

**5. b** — *Circumference and speed are both proportional to v, so they cancel.* r = mv⊥/qB grows in exact proportion to the speed, so the circumference 2πr does too; the lap time T = 2πr/v⊥ = 2πm/qB therefore has no v in it. A faster particle has further to travel and travels proportionately faster, arriving at the same moment. This isochronism is what lets a cyclotron run at one fixed frequency. (a) is a true statement used as a non sequitur — "no work" fixes the *speed* of a given particle, but the question compares particles of **different** speeds, about which no-work says nothing. (c) is empty: the field is uniform in both cases, yet T also depends on m and q, so "fixed by the field alone" is false. (d) is a pseudo-derivation whose arithmetic does not work: setting qvB = mv²/r cancels **one** power of v, leaving r ∝ v, and the second cancellation happens only when you go on to divide the circumference by the speed.

**6. a** — *q cancels from qE = qvB, and m never appears.* The balance condition is qE = qvB, and the common factor q divides out, leaving **v = E/B** — no charge, no mass, no sign. Reverse the sign of the charge and **both** forces reverse together, so the balance survives. That is what makes it a *speed* filter rather than a species filter. (b) is false about the electric force, which is **qE regardless of v** — and the whole mechanism depends on exactly that asymmetry: one force depends on speed and the other does not, so the two can balance at one speed only. (c) invents a mass dependence and then a compensation for it; neither force involves m. (d) describes an apparatus that would have to be retuned constantly, which is precisely the labour the selector saves.

**7. b** — *The wire law is the charge law added up.* An element dl of a wire carrying free charge of linear density λ drifting at v holds dq = λ dl, and feels dF = (dq)v × B = (λ dl)v × B. Since **I = λv**, this is **dF = I dl × B**, integrating for a straight wire in a uniform field to F = I L × B, magnitude BIL sin θ. It is the same physics, counted per unit length instead of per particle. (a) confuses the two halves of magnetostatics: Ampère's law says what a current **produces**; the Lorentz force says what a field **does** to a current. (c) makes two laws where there is one, and would leave no account of why the constants match. (d) misuses Newton's third law, which relates the forces between two currents and cannot by itself produce the expression BIL sin θ.

**8. c** — *The 1/c is Gaussian, not SI.* In Gaussian (cgs) units the magnetic force is written q(v/c) × B and the current-element force (I/c) dl × B, because B is defined so that E and B share the same dimensions. In **SI** — which every Miva numerical question uses — there is **no c**: F = qv × B and dF = I dl × B. Dividing an SI answer by 3×10⁸ makes it wrong by that factor. (a) invents a unit conversion for the current, which is measured in amperes on both sides of the equation. (b) confuses the speed of the charges with the constant c: the drift speed in a copper wire is under a millimetre per second, and the cgs formula carries its c whatever the drift speed. (d) is the most costly answer: the two forms are **not** dimensionally interchangeable, and mixing systems is what produces answers wrong by eight orders of magnitude.

**9.** *Concept: the no-work property of the magnetic force, and what it does and does not forbid.* The magnetic force is **F = qv × B**, and a cross product is perpendicular to both its factors — in particular **F ⊥ v at every instant**. Work is W = ∫F·dl, and the displacement dl is always along v, so the integrand F·dl is identically zero: the instantaneous power P = F·v = 0. Hence the **speed** and the **kinetic energy** of the particle are exactly conserved, no matter how strong the field or how long the particle stays in it. What is **not** conserved is the **momentum**: p = mv is a vector, and its direction turns continuously — over half a revolution it reverses completely. Nor is the velocity conserved, for the same reason. "Does no work" is **not** "exerts no force": there is a real force of magnitude qvB, easily large enough to bend a proton beam round a 27 km ring. It is also **not** "produces no acceleration": the acceleration a = qvB/m is real and is what curves the path — it is a **centripetal** acceleration, changing the direction of v and not its magnitude, and confusing "acceleration" with "speeding up" is precisely the error the topic is designed to expose. A **cyclotron** therefore does not use the magnetic field to add energy at all. The field's whole job is to bend the protons round semicircles inside the dees and — because T = 2πm/qB is independent of speed — to bring them back to the gap at strictly regular intervals. The energy is supplied entirely by the **electric field** across that gap: each time the proton crosses, the alternating voltage has reversed so that qE accelerates it again, adding qV of kinetic energy per crossing. The radius grows as r = mv/qB with each boost, and after many hundreds of crossings the proton spirals out at many MeV. Every joule of that came from **E**, and none of it from **B**.

**10.** *Concept: the geometry of v × B and the effect of the sign of q.* A cross product has two defining properties. **Magnitude** |v × B| = vB sin θ, θ being the angle between the vectors: it is **zero when they are parallel or antiparallel** (θ = 0° or 180°, sin θ = 0) and **greatest when they are perpendicular** (θ = 90°, sin θ = 1). **Direction**: v × B is **perpendicular to the plane containing v and B**, with the sense given by the right hand. Both facts about the magnetic force follow immediately: a charge moving along a field line feels nothing and travels straight, while a charge moving across the field feels the full qvB; and the force is perpendicular to **v** (which is why it does no work) and perpendicular to **B** (which is why motion along B is never affected). Note that the deck's RHR-1 — thumb along **v**, fingers along **B**, force out of the **palm** — is a statement of that same sense, and it gives the force on a **positive** charge. **Reversing the sign of q reverses F**, because q multiplies the whole vector: for an electron, compute the positive-charge answer and then turn it round. The two apparently conflicting observations are reconciled by noticing that **F depends on the product qv**, not on q or v separately. An electron and a proton fired the **same** way have opposite q and the same v, so qv is opposite and the forces are opposite: they curve apart. An electron beam moving one way and a positive-ion beam moving the **other** way have opposite q **and** opposite v, and the two reversals cancel — qv is the same for both, so the force is the same and they curve the **same** way. This is exactly why a conventional current to the right and an electron flow to the left are physically the same current and feel the same force.

**11.** *Concept: the crossed-field selector — one speed-dependent force against one speed-independent force.* Arrange **E** and **B** perpendicular to each other and both perpendicular to the beam. A particle of charge q and speed v then feels an electric force **qE**, whose size does **not** depend on v, and a magnetic force **qvB**, whose size is **proportional to v**; the geometry is set so that the two point in **opposite** directions. Undeflected passage requires them to be equal: **qE = qvB ⟹ v = E/B**. Two features of that condition carry all the physics. First, **q cancels**, and it cancels including its sign — reversing the charge reverses both forces together, so the balance is untouched; electrons and positive ions of the same speed both go straight through. Second, **m never appears at all**, since neither force involves the mass. The selected speed is therefore a property of the **apparatus**, not of the particle. Because one force grows with v and the other does not, the balance holds at exactly **one** speed. A particle **faster** than E/B has qvB > qE, so the **magnetic** force wins and it is deflected towards the side the magnetic force points. A **slower** particle has qvB < qE, so the **electric** force wins and it is deflected the other way. Both miss the exit slit, and only the selected speed survives. To turn this into a **mass spectrometer**, pass the emerging monoenergetic-in-speed beam into a second region containing **B alone**. There the path is a circle of radius **r = mv/(qB′)**, and since v is now known and fixed by the first stage, the radius measures **m/q** directly: heavier ions of the same charge sweep proportionally larger circles and strike the detector further along. The division of labour is the point — the first stage removes the speed spread that would otherwise blur the mass measurement, and the second stage separates by mass.

**12.** *Concept: the transverse geometry of an electromagnetic wave, and c = fλ.* An electromagnetic wave is a self-sustaining pair of oscillating fields. A **changing electric field** generates a **magnetic field**, and a **changing magnetic field** generates an **electric field**; each field's rate of change acts as the source of the other, so once launched — for example by the oscillating current in a transmitting aerial — the disturbance regenerates itself indefinitely and travels away. The generation is by a **cross-product** relation, so the field produced is at right angles to the one producing it: hence **E ⊥ B**, and both are perpendicular to the direction of travel, which is the direction of **E × B**. The wave is therefore **transverse**, with **no component of either field along the direction of propagation** — the standard wrong picture, in which E oscillates back and forth along the line of travel, describes a longitudinal wave such as sound and cannot be an electromagnetic wave at all. The speed in vacuum is fixed by the electric and magnetic properties of free space, **c = 3.00×10⁸ m/s**, and is the same for every electromagnetic wave whatever its frequency. Since the wave advances one wavelength in one period, **c = fλ**, and because c is a constant, **f and λ are inversely proportional**: fix one and the other is determined. The **only** physical difference between a gamma ray and a radio wave is that frequency — and the quantities that follow from it. Both are the same kind of transverse E–B disturbance travelling at the same speed. The gamma ray has an enormously higher frequency and correspondingly shorter wavelength, and therefore an enormously larger **energy per photon**, E = hf = hc/λ: a few MeV against a fraction of a microelectronvolt. That single difference is what makes gamma rays ionising and radio waves harmless, and it is also why radio waves diffract round buildings (wavelengths of metres) while gamma rays travel in essentially straight lines (wavelengths smaller than an atom).
