# Monday — GST_112 fast-hour check

*The nine Makers of Nigeria: their parties, offices, dates and credited achievements.*
*5 questions, straight after the hour. Score out of 5.*

**1.** A lawyer who began practising in Lagos in 1965 took on many cases for people who could not pay, was called the people's lawyer, and from his sick bed sought to reopen the Dele Giwa parcel-bomb case. Who was he?
A. Wole Soyinka
B. Gani Fawehinmi
C. Herbert Macaulay
D. Chinua Achebe

**2.** Which of the following pairs a Maker of Nigeria with the achievement credited to him or her?
A. Chinua Achebe: Nobel Prize for Literature, 1986
B. Obafemi Awolowo: last governor-general of Nigeria, 1960 to 1963
C. Funmilayo Ransome-Kuti: founder of the NNDP in 1923
D. Fela Anikulapo-Kuti: creator of Afrobeat in the early 1970s

**3.** Put these events in the order they happened: (A) Nnamdi Azikiwe becomes Nigeria's first elected president; (B) Herbert Macaulay founds the NNDP; (C) Wole Soyinka receives the Nobel Prize for Literature; (D) Obafemi Awolowo becomes first premier of the Western Region.
A. B, D, A, C
B. B, A, D, C
C. D, B, A, C
D. B, D, C, A

**4.** Which of the following statements about Nnamdi Azikiwe is correct?
A. He founded the NNDP, Nigeria's first political party, in 1923
B. He was the first premier of the Western Region from 1954 to 1960
C. He was the last governor-general from 1960 to 1963 and the first elected president from 1963 to 1966
D. He headed the Biafra Broadcasting Service during the civil war

**5.** What does the term Makers of Nigeria refer to?
A. Key historical figures who contributed to Nigeria's journey to independence and development
B. Members of the Nigerian diaspora who send remittances home
C. The founders of Nigeria's first political party
D. Musicians such as Burna Boy, Wizkid, Davido and Rema who draw positive attention to Nigeria

---

## Answers

**1. B** — *Gani Fawehinmi's credited achievements.* Gani Fawehinmi started practising in Lagos in 1965, became known as the people's lawyer for his pro bono work, campaigned for human rights for 40 years, and pressed to reopen the still unsolved 1986 Dele Giwa case. Soyinka is the playwright and Nobel laureate; Macaulay the father of Nigerian nationalism and NNDP founder; Achebe the novelist of Things Fall Apart.

**2. D** — *Matching makers to credited achievements.* Fela Anikulapo-Kuti is credited as the creator of Afrobeat in the early 1970s, using words as protest and resistance. The 1986 Nobel Prize for Literature belongs to Wole Soyinka, the first African to win it. Azikiwe, not Awolowo, was the last governor-general from 1960 to 1963; Awolowo was first premier of the Western Region. The NNDP was founded by Herbert Macaulay in 1923; Funmilayo Ransome-Kuti's organisation was the Federation of Nigerian Women's Societies.

**3. A** — *Chronology of the makers' milestones.* The NNDP was founded in 1923 (B); Awolowo became first premier of the Western Region in 1954 (D); Azikiwe became first elected president in 1963 (A); Soyinka received the Nobel Prize in 1986 (C). The second option puts Azikiwe's presidency before Awolowo's premiership; the third puts Awolowo before Macaulay's 1923 party; the fourth puts Soyinka's Nobel before the First Republic.

**4. C** — *Azikiwe's offices and dates.* Azikiwe served as the last governor-general of Nigeria from 1960 to 1963 and as the country's first elected president from 1963 to 1966 in the First Republic; he entered politics in 1944, co-founded the NCNC and inspired the Zikist movement. The NNDP of 1923 was Macaulay's; the Western Region premiership was Awolowo's; and it was Chinua Achebe who headed the Biafra Broadcasting Service.

**5. A** — *Definition of Makers of Nigeria.* Makers of Nigeria refers to key historical figures who played pivotal roles in the nation's journey to independence and development, drawn from politics, the economy and the creative industry: Macaulay, Awolowo, Azikiwe, Ransome-Kuti, Fela, Achebe, Soyinka, Fawehinmi and Kanu. The Nigerian diaspora and its remittances are a separate topic from the makers. The NNDP founders are only one of the nine, and Burna Boy, Wizkid, Davido and Rema are named as contemporary Nigerians who deserve a mention, not as the makers.
