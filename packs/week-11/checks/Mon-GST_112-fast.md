# Monday — GST_112 fast-hour check

*The nine Makers of Nigeria: their parties, offices, dates and credited achievements.*
*Five questions, straight after the fast hour. Recall only. Score out of 5.*

**1.** Which party did Herbert Macaulay found in 1923, Nigeria's first?
A. The NCNC
B. The Action Group
C. The NNDP
D. The Nigerian Youth Movement

**2.** Funmilayo Ransome-Kuti's 1953 Abeokuta conference produced which body?
A. The FNWS
B. The NCNC
C. The Zikist movement
D. The NNDP

**3.** What office did Nnamdi Azikiwe hold from 1960 to 1963?
A. First elected president
B. Premier of the Western Region
C. Leader of the Action Group
D. Last governor-general

**4.** Wole Soyinka dedicated his 1986 Nobel Prize to whom?
A. Herbert Macaulay
B. Nelson Mandela
C. Dele Giwa
D. Chinua Achebe

**5.** Which achievement does the deck credit to Nwankwo Kanu?
A. Nigeria's 1996 Olympic Gold
B. Creating Afrobeat in the early 1970s
C. Heading the Biafra Broadcasting Service
D. Founding the Zikist movement

## Answers

**1. C** — The NNDP was Macaulay's 1923 party and the first in Nigeria. The NCNC came later, when the NNDP merged with the Nigerian Youth Movement in the late 1930s; the Action Group was Awolowo's.

**2. A** — The Abeokuta conference on women's welfare produced the FNWS. The Zikist movement was inspired by Azikiwe; the NCNC and NNDP are Macaulay-Azikiwe party history, not women's organising.

**3. D** — 1963 is the hinge year: governor-general before it, president after. The Western Region premiership 1954-1960 and the Action Group both belong to Awolowo.

**4. B** — The deck names Mandela, then imprisoned, as the dedicatee. Dele Giwa is the editor killed by a parcel bomb in 1986 whose case Gani Fawehinmi pursued; Achebe is credited with Things Fall Apart, not a Nobel.

**5. A** — Kanu is the sportsman of the nine. Afrobeat in the early 1970s is Fela; the Biafra Broadcasting Service is Achebe; the Zikist movement traces to Azikiwe.
