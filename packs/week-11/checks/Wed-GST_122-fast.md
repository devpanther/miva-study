# Wednesday — GST_122 fast-hour check

*Hearing versus listening, the six areas of importance, and the strategy and competency lists.*
*Five questions, straight after the fast hour. Recall only. Score out of 5.*

**1.** The deck describes hearing as which pair of qualities?
A. Passive and involuntary
B. Active and voluntary
C. Conscious and interpretive
D. Empathetic and reflective

**2.** Which importance-of-listening area names finding common ground without interruption?
A. Building Relationships
B. Conflict Resolution
C. Effective Communication
D. Avoiding Miscommunication

**3.** Which listed strategy means understanding the speaker's emotions?
A. Active listening
B. Note-taking
C. Paraphrasing
D. Empathetic listening

**4.** Respecting communication differences across cultures is which named competency?
A. Active Engagement
B. Critical Listening
C. Cultural Competence
D. Continuous Improvement

**5.** Which case-study methodology heading covers regular self-assessment?
A. Reflective Practice
B. Cultural Awareness
C. Practical Strategies
D. Nonverbal Communication

## Answers

**1. A** — The headline contrast makes hearing passive and involuntary and listening active and voluntary, needing conscious effort to interpret. If a question says involuntary, the answer is hearing.

**2. B** — Conflict Resolution lets parties express concerns without interruption and identify common ground. Building Relationships is about trust and rapport, and Avoiding Miscommunication is about preventing misunderstanding.

**3. D** — Empathetic listening is understanding the speaker's emotions and expressing empathy. Active listening is demonstrating engagement through eye contact and verbal cues; paraphrasing restates a point in your own words.

**4. C** — The deck calls recognising and respecting cross-cultural differences cultural competence, an essential skill for effective listening. The other three are separate items on the same six-competency list.

**5. A** — Reflective Practice is regular self-assessment to identify areas for improvement. Cultural Awareness covers cross-cultural differences, Practical Strategies lists the six techniques, and Nonverbal Communication is body language and facial expressions.
