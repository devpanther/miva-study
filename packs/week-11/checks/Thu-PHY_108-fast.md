# Thursday — PHY_108 fast-hour check

*Finding the permeability of free space from a circular loop's axial field: formula, apparatus, readings, gradient.*
*Five questions, straight after the fast hour. Recall only. Score out of 5.*

**1.** Plotting B against the factor I R squared over 2(R squared + z squared) to the 3/2, the gradient equals what?
A. The loop current I
B. The loop radius R
C. The permeability of free space
D. The magnetic flux through the loop

**2.** At the centre of the loop, z = 0, the field reduces to which expression?
A. mu-nought I / 2R
B. mu-nought I / 2 pi r
C. mu-nought n I
D. mu-nought I R / 2

**3.** What value is quoted for the permeability of free space?
A. 8.85 x 10 to the -12 F/m
B. 6.63 x 10 to the -34 J.s
C. 9.8 m/s squared
D. 4 pi x 10 to the -7 H/m

**4.** Which listed item actually reads the flux density in the experiment?
A. The ammeter
B. The tesla meter
C. The DC power supply
D. The circular loop

**5.** How many readings are taken per coil, and over what range of z?
A. 10 readings, z = 0 to 18 cm
B. 38 readings, z = 0 to 38 cm
C. 5 readings, z = 0 to 10 cm
D. 20 readings, z = 0 to 38 cm

## Answers

**1. C** — The line is B = mu-nought times x through the origin, so the slope is mu-nought in T.m/A = H/m. If the factor is computed without I, the slope is mu-nought times I instead.

**2. A** — Setting z = 0 in the working formula gives mu-nought I over 2R, the largest value on the axis. The second option is the long straight wire and the third the solenoid; neither is this experiment.

**3. D** — That is about 1.257 x 10 to the -6, quoted as T.m/A in the theory slide and H/m in the summary; the units are equivalent. The first option is the permittivity of free space, a different constant.

**4. B** — The movable magnetic probe sits on the axis and the tesla meter displays B, logged in nT. The ammeter reads the loop current I and the DC supply drives it.

**5. D** — Steps of 2 cm from z = 0 to z = 38 cm give 20 points, the spread needed for a straight-line fit that averages out scatter. The whole set is then repeated for coils of different radius.
