# GST_112 — Week 11

*Nigerian Peoples and Culture · Week 11 (16 – 22 Nov 2026)*

## Most likely to be examined

1. **Herbert Macaulay = "father of Nigerian nationalism"**; founded the **NNDP in 1923** — Nigeria's **first political party**, whose members were the first to sit in the legislative council.
2. **NNDP → NCNC**: NNDP held sway until the **late 1930s**, when it joined the **Nigerian Youth Movement** to form the **National Council of Nigeria and the Cameroons (NCNC)**.
3. **Awolowo = first premier of the Western Region, 1954–1960**; foremost federalist; led the **Action Group (AG)**; his federal-constitution campaign was realised in the **1954 Lyleton [Lyttleton] Constitution**.
4. **Azikiwe's two offices**: **last governor-general 1960–1963**, then **first elected president 1963–1966** (First Republic). Joined politics **1944**; co-founded the NCNC; inspired the **Zikist movement**.
5. **Funmilayo Ransome-Kuti — "Lioness of Lisabi"**; the **1953 Abeokuta conference** on women's welfare produced the **Federation of Nigerian Women's Societies (FNWS)**. Raid on Fela's Lagos home **18 Feb 1977**; thrown from a **second-story window at 76**; died **13 Apr 1978**.
6. **Soyinka's dates**: imprisoned **1967** (a 33-year-old graduate) for **22 months**; fled in **1994** on a treason charge; **Nobel Prize for Literature 1986 — first African**, dedicated to **Nelson Mandela**.
7. **Achebe**: *Things Fall Apart* (**1958**), most widely read book in modern African literature; head of the **Biafra Broadcasting Service**; **PRP deputy national vice president in 1983**; *The Trouble with Nigeria*.
8. **Fela = creator of Afrobeat in the early 1970s**; **Kanu = 1996 Olympic Gold**, beating **Brazil and Argentina** on the road to Gold.

## Commonly confused

- **Macaulay vs Azikiwe on the NCNC** — Macaulay's NNDP *merged into* it in the late 1930s; **Azikiwe co-founded** it. Both are tied to the NCNC, only one founded the NNDP (Macaulay, 1923).
- **Azikiwe's two date ranges** — **1960–1963 governor-general** (the *last*), **1963–1966 president** (the *first elected*). Swapping them is the classic trap; note 1963 is the hinge year.
- **1954 does double duty for Awolowo** — start of his premiership (1954–1960) *and* the Lyleton Constitution. Don't attach 1954 to Macaulay (1923) or to independence.
- **Funmilayo's two dates** — raid **18 Feb 1977**, death **13 Apr 1978**. Her *dates* are 1900–1978; the raid year is not the death year.
- **Funmilayo vs Fela** — mother (1900–1978, activist, FNWS) and son (1938–1997, Afrobeat). The raid was on **Fela's** residence; she was the casualty.
- **Fela vs Gani, both born 1938** — Fela died 1997, Gani died 2009.
- **Soyinka's 1986 (Nobel) vs 1967 (jailed, 22 months) vs 1994 (fled)**; Achebe's **1958** (*Things Fall Apart*) vs **1983** (PRP deputy national vice president). Don't give Achebe a Nobel — the deck credits the Nobel only to Soyinka.
- **Gani's 1965 (began practice in Lagos) vs 1986 (Dele Giwa parcel bombing) vs 2009 (death)** — and the **40 years** of campaigning is a duration, not a date.
- **Living vs dead**: only **Soyinka (1934– )** and **Kanu (1976– )** are given as "Present".
