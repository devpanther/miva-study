# Thursday — MTH_102 nightly check

*Week 5 differentiation treated as problems: reading an expression and deciding which rule it needs, then executing it correctly. Covers differentiation from first principles on a simple algebraic function, the power rule with ordinary and negative or fractional indices, product and quotient rules with polynomial factors, the chain rule on a bracket raised to a power and on a root, implicit differentiation of a relation in x and y, and parametric differentiation via dy/dx = (dy/dt)/(dx/dt). Also covers checking a finished derivative for plausibility and diagnosing exactly which step a wrong answer omitted.*
*Sit cold, notes closed, about 15 minutes. Score out of 12.*

a) 2x
b) x + h

**3.** Which route is the correct one for differentiating y = (2x + 3)/(x squared - 1)?
a) Apply the power rule to the numerator and the denominator separately and then divide the results

**6.** A student writes dy/dx = 4(x squared + 3) cubed for y = (x squared + 3) to the power 4. Which single step was omitted?
