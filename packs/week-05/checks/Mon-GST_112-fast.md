# Monday — GST_112 fast-hour check

*The coup sequence from January 1966, the countercoup, the 1975 coup, the end of the civil war and foreign backing.*
*Five questions, straight after the fast hour. Recall only. Score out of 5.*

**1.** Who led the five majors in the coup of 15 January 1966?
A. Emmanuel Ifeajuna
B. Adewale Ademoyega
C. Chukwuma Kaduna Nzeogwu
D. Johnson Aguiyi-Ironsi

**2.** Which decree did Gowon repeal immediately after the July 1966 countercoup?
A. Decree No. 34, the Unification Decree
B. The Richards Constitution
C. The Aburi Accord
D. The state creation decree

**3.** Who led the bloodless coup of 29 July 1975 that removed Gowon?
A. Murtala Muhammed
B. Olusegun Obasanjo
C. Buka Suka Dimka
D. Joseph Nanven Garba

**4.** Who surrendered to Gowon in Lagos on 12 January 1970?
A. Odumegwu Ojukwu
B. Philip Effiong
C. Nwafor Orizu
D. Ladoke Akintola

**5.** Which power supplied the Federal Military Government with aircraft and advisers?
A. France
B. Portugal
C. The USSR
D. The United Kingdom

## Answers

**1. C** — Major Chukwuma Kaduna Nzeogwu led the five majors. Ifeajuna and Ademoyega were two of the other majors; Ironsi did not plot the coup, he was the officer power devolved to afterwards.

**2. A** — Ironsi's Decree No. 34, the Unification Decree, was one of the gaffes that provoked the north, and Gowon repealed it at once. The Richards Constitution introduced regionalism, Aburi was a failed negotiation, and state creation came later in 1967.

**3. D** — Colonel Joseph Nanven Garba led it; the plotters then appointed Murtala Muhammed Head of State with Obasanjo as deputy. Dimka led the failed 1976 coup that killed Murtala.

**4. B** — Ojukwu fled to Ivory Coast; Effiong surrendered in Lagos. Orizu was the acting Senate President who handed power to Ironsi in 1966, and Akintola was killed in the January coup.

**5. C** — The USSR backed the federal side with aircraft and advisers. France and Portugal supplied Biafra; the UK, like the US, chose neutrality.
