# Wednesday — GST_122 fast-hour check

*The three stages of the writing process, and naming pre-writing techniques and post-writing activities.*
*5 questions, straight after the hour. Score out of 5.*

**1.** Before shooting a short film, Funke sketches each scene in order on a series of panels so that the narrative structure is organised before any writing begins. Which pre-writing technique is this?
A. Storyboarding
B. Outlining
C. Mind mapping
D. Listing

**2.** Having finished a news article, Ada works through it removing grammatical errors, improving sentence structure and making the writing style consistent, without changing what the article says. Which post-writing activity is this?
A. Revision
B. Editing for grammar and style
C. Proofreading
D. Peer review

**3.** Put these activities in the order the writing process requires: (1) exchange drafts with a classmate for feedback; (2) list the similarities and differences between two historical figures; (3) compose the essay, giving each paragraph a topic sentence; (4) submit the final essay.
A. 2, 3, 1, 4
B. 2, 1, 3, 4
C. 3, 2, 1, 4
D. 2, 3, 4, 1

**4.** Which of the following is NOT one of the three divergent thinking methods named for pre-writing?
A. Mind mapping
B. Free-writing
C. Concept clustering
D. Outlining

**5.** Which stage of the writing process is defined as a series of strategic activities aimed at generating and organising ideas, establishing a purpose, and constructing a preliminary framework?
A. During-writing
B. Post-writing
C. Pre-writing
D. Proofreading

---

## Answers

**1. A** — *Naming a pre-writing technique from a scenario.* Storyboarding is a visual plan of the sequence of scenes, used by a filmmaker to organise narrative structure.

Outlining sets out key points, evidence and logical flow in words; mind mapping draws branches of connected ideas around a topic; listing writes out items such as similarities and differences.

**2. B** — *Revising versus editing versus proofreading.* Editing for grammar and style works at the language level: eliminating grammatical errors, improving sentence structure and ensuring consistency of style.

Revision reworks content and structure, such as the introduction or paragraph order; proofreading catches only surface errors such as typos, misspellings and punctuation; peer review is feedback from a fellow student.

**3. A** — *Ordering the three stages of the writing process.* The stages run pre-writing, during-writing, post-writing. Listing (2) is pre-writing, composing (3) is the writing phase, peer review (1) is post-writing, and publishing or submitting (4) is the final step that ends the process.

'2, 1, 3, 4' puts peer review before a draft exists; '3, 2, 1, 4' writes before planning; '2, 3, 4, 1' submits before revising.

**4. D** — *Divergent thinking methods in pre-writing.* The three divergent thinking methods are mind mapping, free-writing and concept clustering.

Outlining is a pre-writing technique, which is why it is a tempting choice, but it is a structuring roadmap of key points rather than one of the three divergent thinking methods.

**5. C** — *Definition of pre-writing.* Pre-writing is the phase that precedes actual composition and involves generating and organising ideas, establishing a purpose and constructing a preliminary framework.

During-writing is the composing phase; post-writing is the revising, editing and refining done after the initial draft; proofreading is one post-writing activity, not a stage.
