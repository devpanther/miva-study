# GST_112 — Week 5 Question Set (sit 7 days later)

*Sit this during Saturday catch-up in Week 6, not this week. Notes closed.*

## Multiple choice (12)

**1.** Nigeria gained independence from British colonial rule on:
A) October 1, 1963  B) October 1, 1960  C) January 15, 1966  D) September 1, 1960

**2.** The First Republic's 1964 election crisis is remembered as a contest between:
A) NPC vs NCNC  B) UPGA vs NNA  C) AG vs NPC  D) NCNC vs AG

**3.** The first coup (January 15, 1966) was led by five majors. Which of the following was NOT one of the five named in the source?
A) Nzeogwu  B) Anuforo  C) T. Y. Danjuma  D) Ifeajuna

**4.** After the first coup, Nwafor Orizu transferred power to:
A) Yakubu Gowon  B) Johnson Aguiyi-Ironsi  C) Murtala Muhammed  D) Chukwuemeka Ojukwu

**5.** Ironsi's unification decree, later repealed by Gowon, was known as:
A) Decree No. 24  B) Decree No. 34  C) Decree No. 43  D) Decree No. 12

**6.** The countercoup that killed Aguiyi-Ironsi in Ibadan took place on:
A) July 29, 1966  B) January 15, 1966  C) May 30, 1967  D) July 29, 1975

**7.** Ojukwu declared the East's independence as Biafra on:
A) July 29, 1966  B) January 12, 1970  C) May 30, 1967  D) April 20, 1990

**8.** How many states did Gowon's government create out of the existing regions at the start of the war?
A) 3  B) 12  C) 19  D) 36

**9.** Which OAU member states formally recognised Biafra in 1968?
A) Kenya, Uganda, Ghana, Egypt  B) Tanzania, Gabon, Ivory Coast, Zambia  C) South Africa, Rhodesia, Angola, Mozambique  D) Senegal, Mali, Niger, Chad

**10.** The Biafran officer who formally surrendered to Gowon in Lagos on January 12, 1970 was:
A) Chukwuemeka Ojukwu  B) Philip Effiong  C) Joseph Garba  D) Gideon Orkar

**11.** Which coup was a FAILED attempt in which Major Gideon Orkar seized the Federal Radio Corporation of Nigeria and announced the secession of southern Nigeria?
A) The 1976 Dimka coup  B) The 1990 Orkar coup  C) The 1993 Abacha coup  D) The 1985 Babangida coup

**12.** General Sani Abacha overthrew the interim government of Ernest Shonekan on:
A) November 17, 1993  B) June 12, 1993  C) July 29, 1975  D) January 1970

## Short answer (3)

**13.** List, in order, the Heads of State from Gowon to Abacha, naming who overthrew (or succeeded) whom.

**14.** Explain what the Pogrom was, give its casualty range, and state what it led Ojukwu to do.

**15.** Name the five majors of the first coup and the officer to whom power was eventually transferred.

## Answers

**1. B** — independence was **October 1, 1960**. (C is the date of the first coup, six years later — don't confuse the two October/January dates.)
**2. B** — the 1964 crisis was between **UPGA and NNA**; NPC/NCNC/AG were parties from the earlier constitutional era (Week 4), not the 1964 electoral blocs.
**3. C** — **T. Y. Danjuma** is a real Nigerian general but is not one of the five majors the source names. The source's five are **Nzeogwu, Ifeajuna, Okafor, Anuforo, Ademoyega**.
**4. B** — power passed to **Major-General Johnson Aguiyi-Ironsi**, the highest-ranking officer. Gowon only became Head of State later, after the countercoup.
**5. B** — **Decree No. 34**, Ironsi's unification decree, which Gowon repealed on taking power.
**6. A** — **July 29, 1966**. (D reuses the same calendar date nine years later — that's the 1975 coup against Gowon, not the 1966 countercoup against Ironsi.)
**7. C** — **May 30, 1967**. (A and B are the countercoup and the war's end respectively; don't merge Biafra's declaration with either.)
**8. B** — **12 states**, three of them carved from the Eastern Region and only one of those predominantly Igbo.
**9. B** — **Tanzania, Gabon, Ivory Coast, and Zambia** formally recognised Biafra in 1968.
**10. B** — **Philip Effiong** surrendered to Gowon in Lagos on January 12, 1970. (Ojukwu had already fled to Ivory Coast by then.)
**11. B** — the **1990 Orkar coup** was the failed attempt against Babangida; the 1976 Dimka coup was a separate failed attempt, fourteen years earlier, that killed Murtala Muhammed instead.
**12. A** — **November 17, 1993**, about three months into Shonekan's interim presidency. (B is the date of the annulled election that led to the interim government in the first place.)
**13.** **Gowon** (countercoup, Jul 29 1966) → **Murtala Muhammed** (Garba-led coup, Jul 29 1975) → **Obasanjo** (after Dimka's failed 1976 coup killed Murtala) → power returned to civilian **Shagari** → **Buhari** (coup, 1983) → **Babangida** (coup, 1985) → **Shonekan** (Babangida steps aside, 1993) → **Abacha** (coup, Nov 17 1993).
**14.** The Pogrom was the massacre of **80,000 to 100,000 easterners** in the north following the countercoup; it triggered revenge killings of northerners in the east, a mass return of Igbos to the East, and led **Ojukwu to pursue secession** (Gowon, by contrast, was determined to keep the status quo).
**15.** The five majors: **Nzeogwu (Kaduna), Ifeajuna, Okafor, Anuforo, and Ademoyega**. Power was eventually transferred (via acting Senate President Nwafor Orizu) to **Major-General Johnson Aguiyi-Ironsi**.
