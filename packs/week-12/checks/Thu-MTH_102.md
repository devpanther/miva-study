# Thursday — MTH_102 nightly check

*The same applications worked as problems: recovering velocity and position from acceleration, distance versus displacement, work done by a variable force and by a spring, fluid force on a submerged plate, cost from marginal cost, consumer and producer surplus at equilibrium, and accumulated pollutant from an exponential rate.*
*Sit cold, notes closed, about 15 minutes. Score out of 12.*

**1.** A body has acceleration a(t) = 6t + 4 m/s² and velocity v(0) = 5 m/s. Its velocity at t = 3 s is
a) 39 m/s
b) 44 m/s
c) 71 m/s
d) 22 m/s

**2.** A particle moves with velocity v(t) = t² − 4t + 3 m/s. The total distance it travels between t = 0 and t = 4 s is
a) 3 m
b) 4/3 m
c) 8/3 m
d) 4 m

**3.** A force of 12 N holds a spring stretched 0.3 m beyond its natural length. The work done in stretching it from its natural length to 0.5 m is
a) 5 J
b) 6 J
c) 10 J
d) 20 J

**4.** A rectangular plate 2 m wide and 3 m deep hangs vertically in water with its top edge 1 m below the surface (ρg = 9800 N/m³). The fluid force on one face is
a) 58.8 kN
b) 73.5 kN
c) 147 kN
d) 235.2 kN

**5.** A firm's marginal cost is MC(q) = 3q² − 4q + 10 (₦ per unit) and its fixed cost is ₦50. The additional cost of raising output from q = 2 to q = 5 is
a) ₦51
b) ₦105
c) ₦125
d) ₦155

**6.** In a market with demand p = 50 − 2q and supply p = 5 + q, the consumer surplus at equilibrium is
a) 225
b) 337.5
c) 450
d) 112.5

**7.** In the same market (demand p = 50 − 2q, supply p = 5 + q), the producer surplus at equilibrium is
a) 225
b) 300
c) 337.5
d) 112.5

**8.** A factory discharges a pollutant into a river at a rate r(t) = 60e^{0.1t} kg/day. The total mass discharged over the first 10 days is
a) 6(e − 1) ≈ 10.3 kg
b) 60(e − 1) ≈ 103 kg
c) 600(e − 1) ≈ 1031 kg
d) 600e ≈ 1631 kg

**9. (explain why)** A body has acceleration a(t) = 6t − 12 m/s², with v(0) = 9 m/s and s(0) = 0, over 0 ≤ t ≤ 4 s. Find v(t) and s(t), then give both the displacement and the total distance travelled. Explain why the two differ and where the interval had to be split.

**10. (explain why)** A spring has natural length 20 cm, and a force of 15 N holds it at 25 cm. Find the work done in stretching it from 25 cm to 35 cm. State the value of k and explain why the answer is not (15 N) × (0.10 m).

**11. (explain why)** A vertical triangular gate has a horizontal base 4 m wide whose depth is 2 m, and its apex points downwards 3 m below that base (so the gate spans depths 2 m to 5 m). Taking ρg = 9800 N/m³, write the width w(h) of the strip at depth h, set up the force integral and evaluate it. Check your answer using the centroid.

**12. (explain why)** A firm has MR(q) = 60 − 2q and MC(q) = 12 + q (₦ per unit), with fixed cost ₦300. Find the output that maximises profit, and the increase in profit obtained in going from q = 0 to that output. Explain why the fixed cost does not change the optimal output, and say what the fixed cost does change.
