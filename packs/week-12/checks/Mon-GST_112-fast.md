# Monday — GST_112 fast-hour check

*Social vices: crime versus deviance, the theory lists, campus cultism and the kidnapping figures.*
*Five questions, straight after the fast hour. Recall only. Score out of 5.*

**1.** What separates crime from deviant behaviour in the deck?
A. Crime is violent, deviance is not
B. Crime violates laws, deviance violates norms and rules
C. Crime is urban, deviance is rural
D. They are interchangeable with no difference

**2.** Which theory holds that deviance is a consequence of material inequality?
A. Labelling Theory
B. Structural Functionalism
C. Social Strain Typology
D. Conflict Theory

**3.** Who founded the Pirates Confraternity at the University of Ibadan?
A. Wole Soyinka
B. Ralph Opara
C. Olumuyiwa Awe
D. Pius Oleghe

**4.** What sentence does the deck give for membership of a cult group?
A. 3 years
B. 10 years
C. 21 years
D. Life imprisonment

**5.** How many kidnapping cases were reported in 2022?
A. 4,600
B. 838
C. 10,249
D. 11,858

## Answers

**1. B** — Crime carries legal consequences such as fines or imprisonment; deviance is the broader category of straying from norms. The deck's own mid-lesson question offers the interchangeable option as the wrong answer.

**2. D** — Conflict Theory blames material inequality. Labelling Theory blames a deviant label applied to the person, Structural Functionalism calls deviance constructive, and Social Strain Typology works from goals and beliefs about attaining them.

**3. A** — Soyinka is named as the founder, with patriotic and philanthropic beliefs, not as a secret cult. Opara, Awe and Oleghe are among the friends listed as co-founders alongside him.

**4. C** — Membership of a cult group, or soliciting members for an unlawful society, draws 21 years. The 3-year felony is for allowing a cult meeting in a residence or structure you own.

**5. A** — Kidnapping quadrupled from 838 in 2018 to 4,600 in 2022. The other two numbers are the armed robbery prison records, which fell from 11,858 in 2013 to 10,249 in 2014.
