## Multiple choice (12)

**1.** Which best defines culture as treated in this course?
A) The economic system of a nation
B) The complex system of traditional behaviours, beliefs, practices and values of a people
C) The political structure of a state
D) The legal code governing a society

**2.** The three components of culture are:
A) Economic, political, social  B) Technological, sociological, ideological  C) Material, spiritual, legal  D) Traditional, modern, contemporary

**3.** A community's farming tools and building techniques belong to which component of culture?
A) Ideological  B) Sociological  C) Technological (material culture)  D) Associational

**4.** The institution of marriage and the norms guiding family life belong to which component of culture?
A) Technological  B) Sociological  C) Ideological  D) Communal

**5.** A people's religious beliefs and value system belong to which component of culture?
A) Technological  B) Sociological  C) Ideological  D) Associational

**6.** In a communal society, social relationships are typically based on:
A) Contract and formal interest  B) Kinship, tradition, and informal ties  C) Elected representation  D) Industrial specialisation

**7.** An associational society is best distinguished by:
A) Strong reliance on kinship ties
B) High division of labour, formal and impersonal interactions, and weaker attachment to tradition
C) Total absence of any social organisation
D) Total absence of institutions

**8.** Karl Marx's communist vision centred on:
A) Private ownership of the means of production
B) Collective ownership of the means of production and distribution of wealth
C) Monarchical rule
D) Religious governance

**9.** Functionalism views society as:
A) A battlefield of competing classes
B) An organism in which each component plays a role for the whole
C) A random collection of individuals
D) A purely economic unit

**10.** Colonialism's impact on African societies, as covered this week, includes changes to:
A) Governance, culture, and family structures  B) Only the climate  C) Only trade routes  D) Only the education curriculum

**11.** Nation-building, as defined this week, involves:
A) Only military strength
B) Mobilising resources for socio-economic and political development, and constructing a national identity
C) Only writing a constitution
D) Only holding elections

**12.** National integration is best described as:
A) Automatic once independence is achieved
B) The process of unifying diverse groups within a nation — a particular challenge in ethnically diverse countries like Nigeria
C) Identical in meaning to nation-building, with no distinction
D) A purely economic policy

## Short answer (3)

**13.** State the three components of culture and give one example of something that would fall under each.

**14.** Distinguish communal society from associational society along two of the three named dimensions (division of labour, social interactions, traditions).

**15.** Explain why national integration is a particular challenge in Nigeria, referencing the country's ethnic diversity and the impact of colonialism.

## Answer key

**1. B** — *definition of culture* as a complex system of behaviours, beliefs, practices and values.
**2. B** — *the three components*: technological, sociological, ideological.
**3. C** — *technological/material culture* covers tools and physical technology.
**4. B** — *sociological component* covers institutions and norms such as marriage and family.
**5. C** — *ideological component* covers beliefs and values.
**6. B** — *communal society* runs on kinship and informal, tradition-bound ties.
**7. B** — *associational society* runs on formal, impersonal ties and specialised labour.
**8. B** — *Marx's communism*: collective ownership plus distribution of wealth.
**9. B** — *functionalism*: society as an organism, each part playing a role.
**10. A** — *impacts of colonialism*: governance, culture and family structures.
**11. B** — *nation-building*: resource mobilisation plus construction of national identity.
**12. B** — *national integration*: unifying diverse groups, hard in ethnically diverse Nigeria.
**13.** *The three components of culture*: technological (material culture — tools, technology), sociological (institutions and norms — marriage, government), ideological (beliefs and values — religion, moral values).
**14.** *Communal vs associational society*: communal has a low, undifferentiated division of labour, informal kinship-based interactions and strong attachment to tradition; associational has a high, specialised division of labour, formal and impersonal interactions and weaker attachment to tradition.
**15.** *National integration in Nigeria*: the country's many ethnic groups, combined with colonialism's disruption of pre-colonial governance and family structures and the arbitrary grouping of diverse peoples into one territory, make forging a single shared national identity difficult — which is why deliberate government integration initiatives have been necessary.
