# Monday — GST_112 fast-hour check

*Culture's three components, the two society types, functionalism, colonialism's impact and nation-building.*
*Five questions, straight after the fast hour. Recall only. Score out of 5.*

**1.** Which trio names the three fundamental components of culture?
A. Material, spiritual, political
B. Technological, economic, ideological
C. Technological, sociological, ideological
D. Sociological, ideological, historical

**2.** Communal and associational societies are distinguished on which three bases?
A. Division of labour, social interactions, traditions
B. Language, religion, territory
C. Division of labour, wealth, governance
D. Traditions, beliefs, material culture

**3.** Which view sees society as an organism where each part plays a crucial role?
A. Marx's communist society
B. National integration
C. Nation-building
D. Functionalism

**4.** Colonialism is said to have altered which three domains of African societies?
A. Trade, warfare, language
B. Governance, culture, family structures
C. Religion, economy, technology
D. Governance, religion, education

**5.** Nation-building means mobilising resources for development and doing what else?
A. Removing ethnic diversity
B. Enforcing a single national language
C. Constructing a national identity
D. Collectivising the means of production

## Answers

**1. C** — The course names technological (material culture), sociological (institutions and norms) and ideological (beliefs and values). The other three swap in economic, political or historical, none of which is one of the three.

**2. A** — The course fixes exactly three: division of labour, social interactions and traditions. The others borrow real course words (traditions, material culture, governance) but are not the stated three.

**3. D** — Functionalism treats society as an organism whose every component serves the whole. Marx instead frames society around collective ownership of the means of production; the other two are processes, not theories of society.

**4. B** — The recap states governance, culture and family structures, with pre-colonial Yoruba communities as the worked example. The other options substitute domains the source never lists.

**5. C** — Both halves are needed: mobilising resources for socio-economic and political development plus constructing a national identity. Collectivising production is Marx; the other two are not part of the definition.
