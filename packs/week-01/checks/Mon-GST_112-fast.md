# Monday — GST_112 fast-hour check

*Culture's three components, the two society types, functionalism, colonialism's impact and nation-building.*
*5 questions, straight after the hour. Score out of 5.*

**1.** A people's hoes, looms, cooking pots and the houses they build are being studied. Which component of culture do these belong to?
A. Technological
B. Sociological
C. Ideological
D. Associational

**2.** Communal and associational societies differ on three bases. Which of the following is NOT one of them?
A. Division of labour
B. Social interactions
C. Religion
D. Traditions

**3.** A sociologist argues that a village's family, market and shrine each do a job that keeps the whole community alive, like the organs of a body. Which perspective is this?
A. Karl Marx's communist vision
B. Nation-building
C. Colonialism
D. Functionalism

**4.** Under foreign rule a pre-colonial Yoruba community had its chiefs replaced, its customs reshaped and its households reorganised. Which three domains of African society does this show colonialism altering?
A. Trade, warfare and language
B. Governance, culture and family structures
C. Religion, economy and technology
D. Education, transport and currency

**5.** Which of the following is the definition of culture?
A. Collective ownership of the means of production and distribution of wealth
B. Mobilising resources for development and constructing a national identity
C. A complex system of traditional behaviours, beliefs, practices and values
D. A grouping of people into communal or associational forms

---

## Answers

**1. A** — *Three components of culture.* Tools, artefacts and built objects are material culture, which is the technological component. Sociological would be the institutions and norms that govern people; ideological would be their beliefs and values. Associational is a type of society, not a component of culture.

**2. C** — *Communal vs associational society.* The three bases are division of labour, social interactions and traditions. Religion is a plausible outsider: it is part of culture, but it is not one of the three bases used to separate the two types of society.

**3. D** — *Functionalism.* Functionalism views society as an organism in which each component plays a crucial role, exactly the organ-of-a-body picture. Marx frames society around collective ownership of the means of production and wealth distribution, not organs and roles. Nation-building is a process of development and identity construction, and colonialism is foreign rule, so neither is a theory of how society works.

**4. B** — *Impact of colonialism on African societies.* Colonialism altered governance (the chiefs), culture (the customs) and family structures (the households). The other options name real colonial changes in places, but they are not the three domains identified for African societies, and each swaps in at least one domain that is not on the list.

**5. C** — *Definition of culture.* Culture is a complex system encompassing traditional behaviours, beliefs, practices and values. Collective ownership of the means of production is Karl Marx's communist society; mobilising resources and constructing a national identity is nation-building; grouping people into communal or associational forms describes society, not culture.
