# Wednesday — GST_122 fast-hour check

*The four language skills, their definitions, active versus passive listening, speaking components and writing styles.*
*Five questions, straight after the fast hour. Recall only. Score out of 5.*

**1.** Listening is defined as the ability to do what with spoken language?
A. Hear and repeat it accurately
B. Understand, interpret and respond to it
C. Comprehend, interpret and analyse it
D. Articulate it coherently and effectively

**2.** Background music playing without deep comprehension is the example of what?
A. Active listening
B. Hearing loss of context
C. Passive listening
D. Oral communication

**3.** Which is NOT listed as a component of effective speaking?
A. Clear pronunciation
B. Voice control
C. Body language
D. Reading comprehension

**4.** Which writing style explains a topic without arguing a side?
A. Persuasive
B. Narrative
C. Expository
D. Descriptive

**5.** Extracting key findings from a research paper illustrates which skill?
A. Reading
B. Listening
C. Writing
D. Speaking

## Answers

**1. B** — Listening is understanding, interpreting and responding to spoken language. Comprehending, interpreting and analysing is the definition of reading; articulating coherently is speaking.

**2. C** — Passive listening is merely hearing without deep comprehension, and background music is its set example. Active listening is full engagement, with a lecture as the example.

**3. D** — The components are clear pronunciation, appropriate tone, audience engagement, voice control, body language and delivery. Reading comprehension is a separate skill, not part of speaking.

**4. C** — Expository writing informs or explains neutrally. Persuasive takes a side, narrative tells a sequence of events, descriptive paints a static picture in detail.

**5. A** — Reading is the written incoming skill, and the research paper is its set example. Summarising a TED Talk is listening, the job interview answer is speaking, the persuasive essay is writing.
