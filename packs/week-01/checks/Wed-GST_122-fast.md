# Wednesday — GST_122 fast-hour check

*The four language skills, their definitions, active versus passive listening, speaking components and writing styles.*
*5 questions, straight after the hour. Score out of 5.*

**1.** Bola sits through a two-hour seminar, follows every argument and can restate the main points afterwards. Dayo has the radio on while cooking and cannot say what was discussed. Classify Bola and Dayo in that order.
A. Active listening; passive listening
B. Passive listening; active listening
C. Reading comprehension; active listening
D. Active listening; oral communication

**2.** Kemi studies a novel and works out how its symbolism shapes the story, paying attention to the main ideas, the context and the subtext. Which skill is she exercising?
A. Active listening
B. Expository writing
C. Reading comprehension
D. Oral communication

**3.** A newspaper column titled 'Why You Must Exercise Daily' opens by stating the writer's position and then piles up reasons to make the reader agree. Which writing style is this?
A. Descriptive
B. Narrative
C. Expository
D. Persuasive

**4.** Which of the following is NOT one of the four key takeaways of the comprehension passage 'The Dangers of Faking Mental Health Issues'?
A. Undermining Legitimacy
B. Increasing Hospital Funding
C. Obstructing Access to Support
D. Misallocation of Resources

**5.** Which language skill is defined as the ability to articulate thoughts, ideas and information coherently and effectively?
A. Writing
B. Listening
C. Speaking
D. Reading

---

## Answers

**1. A** — *Active versus passive listening.* Active listening is full engagement, as during a lecture; passive listening is merely hearing without deep comprehension, as with background music. Bola is fully engaged, Dayo is not.

Swapping the two labels reverses the definitions; reading comprehension concerns written text, and oral communication is a speaking skill, not a listening one.

**2. C** — *Classifying a scenario to a language skill.* Effective reading involves understanding main ideas, context and subtext, and analysing a novel's symbolism is the model case of reading comprehension.

Active listening deals with spoken input, expository writing is a writing style for explaining a topic, and oral communication is the delivery side of speaking.

**3. D** — *Identifying a writing style.* The four writing styles are persuasive, descriptive, narrative and expository. A text that takes a side and works to convince the reader is persuasive.

Descriptive writing paints a detailed picture, narrative tells events in sequence, and expository explains a topic neutrally without arguing a side.

**4. B** — *Key takeaways of the mental health comprehension passage.* The four takeaways are Undermining Legitimacy, Obstructing Access to Support, Misallocation of Resources, and Honesty and Understanding. Hospital funding is never mentioned in the passage.

The other three options are all genuine takeaways, so picking one of them means the list was not learned.

**5. C** — *Definition of speaking.* Speaking is the ability to articulate thoughts, ideas and information coherently and effectively.

Writing is the capacity to convey thoughts, information and emotions through written language; listening is to understand, interpret and respond to spoken language; reading is to comprehend, interpret and analyse written text.
