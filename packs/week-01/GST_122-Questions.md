## Multiple choice (12)

**1.** Which two language skills are classified as "receptive"?
a) Speaking and writing  b) Listening and reading  c) Listening and speaking  d) Reading and writing

**2.** Which two language skills are classified as "productive"?
a) Listening and reading  b) Speaking and writing  c) Reading and speaking  d) Listening and writing

**3.** Listening is best defined as:
a) The physical perception of sound waves
b) The ability to understand, interpret, and respond to spoken language
c) The ability to repeat back what was said word for word
d) A component of body language

**4.** What is the key difference between listening and hearing?
a) Hearing requires more concentration than listening
b) They mean exactly the same thing
c) Hearing is passive physical perception of sound, while listening involves understanding and interpreting
d) Listening only applies to lectures, hearing applies to music

**5.** A student listens attentively to a lecture, mentally processing and evaluating the arguments. This is an example of:
a) Passive listening  b) Hearing  c) Active listening  d) Expository writing

**6.** A person has background music playing while working but is not consciously processing the lyrics. This best illustrates:
a) Active listening  b) Passive listening  c) Reading comprehension  d) Descriptive writing

**7.** Speaking, as defined in the lesson, is the ability to:
a) Read written text aloud accurately
b) Articulate thoughts, ideas, and information coherently and effectively
c) Memorise a script word for word
d) Interpret subtext in a conversation

**8.** Which of the following is NOT listed as a component of effective oral communication?
a) Clear pronunciation  b) Voice control and body language  c) Audience engagement  d) Punctuation accuracy

**9.** Reading is defined in the lesson as the ability to:
a) Convey thoughts and emotions through written language
b) Comprehend, interpret, and analyse written text
c) Speak fluently in more than one language
d) Listen actively to spoken instructions

**10.** An essay that tells the story of a student's first day at university, describing events in the order they happened, is an example of which writing style?
a) Expository  b) Persuasive  c) Narrative  d) Descriptive

**11.** An essay that vividly paints a picture of a sunset using rich sensory detail, without necessarily telling a sequence of events, is an example of which writing style?
a) Narrative  b) Descriptive  c) Persuasive  d) Expository

**12.** A piece arguing that a policy should be adopted on the grounds of personal autonomy is best classified as which writing style?
a) Expository  b) Descriptive  c) Narrative  d) Persuasive

## Short answer (3)

**13.** Name the four language skills and state which two are receptive and which two are productive.

**14.** Define active listening and passive listening, and give one example of each from the lesson.

**15.** Name the four writing styles covered in the lesson and give a one-line definition of each.

## Answer key

**1. b** — *receptive skills*: listening and reading take language in.
**2. b** — *productive skills*: speaking and writing produce language.
**3. b** — *definition of listening*: understanding, interpreting and responding to spoken language.
**4. c** — *listening vs hearing*: hearing is passive sound perception; listening adds understanding and response.
**5. c** — *active listening*: full engagement with spoken content.
**6. b** — *passive listening*: sound registering without deep comprehension.
**7. b** — *definition of speaking*: articulating thoughts and ideas coherently and effectively.
**8. d** — *components of effective oral communication*: pronunciation, tone, audience engagement, voice control, body language, delivery — punctuation is a writing concern.
**9. b** — *definition of reading*: comprehending, interpreting and analysing written text.
**10. c** — *narrative writing*: a sequenced account of events.
**11. b** — *descriptive writing*: vivid sensory portrayal of a subject.
**12. d** — *persuasive writing*: arguing a position to convince the reader.
**13.** *The four language skills*: listening, speaking, reading, writing — listening and reading are receptive, speaking and writing are productive.
**14.** *Active vs passive listening*: active listening is full engagement, for example during a lecture; passive listening is merely hearing without deep comprehension, for example background music.
**15.** *The four writing styles*: persuasive (convinces or argues a position), descriptive (vividly portrays a subject through sensory detail), narrative (tells a story or sequence of events), expository (explains or informs, presenting facts or a process).
