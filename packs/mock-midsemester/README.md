## How to sit them

**One unbroken sitting per paper, timed, notes closed, and marked only at the end.** A paper sat in pieces with the summary open measures nothing. If the clock runs out, stop and mark what you have — a truthful 31/50 is worth more than a comfortable 44/50.

These are extra work on top of the Week 5 pack, so spread them over the two weeks rather than cramming a weekend:

```
Week 5  Sat catch-up hour      CSC_106  (40 min)
        Sun, before the recap  GST_112  (40 min)
Week 6  Fri review hour        MTH_102  (60 min)
        Sat catch-up hour      PHY_102  (60 min)
        Sun afternoon          COS_102  (60 min)
        any spare fast hour    PHY_108, GST_122  (40 min each)
```

Do the three 60-minute papers on separate days. Sit each one at the time of day you would sit the real exam, not last thing at night.

## After marking

Every entry in a marking scheme is labelled with the **week** it tests. So don't just total the score — total it *per week*. Three papers all leaking marks on Week 2 questions is a Week 2 problem, not three separate problems, and it tells you exactly which summary to re-read before Week 6.

Each paper's `## How to read your score` section gives the bands for that course. Take the two lowest weeks across all seven papers into the next Sunday recap — those beat the fallback topics in `packs/week-05/Sunday-Recap.md`.

Section B is self-marked against the mark breakdown. Mark it honestly and strictly: award a mark only where the scheme says the mark is earned, not where you feel you knew it.
