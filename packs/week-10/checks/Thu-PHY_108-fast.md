# Thursday — PHY_108 fast-hour check

*Magnetic moment of a bar magnet: tangent law, set-up, eight readings and the graph.*
*Five questions, straight after the fast hour. Recall only. Score out of 5.*

**1.** The tangent law used in this experiment states that:
A. B = B_H sin θ
B. B = B_H tan θ
C. B = B_H cos θ
D. B = B_H / tan θ

**2.** Which way must the metre stick be laid for this experiment?
A. East-west
B. North-south
C. Vertically
D. Along the magnetic meridian

**3.** How many deflection angles are recorded at each distance d?
A. Two
B. Four
C. Six
D. Eight

**4.** Magnetic moment M and pole strength m are related by:
A. M = mL
B. M = 2mL
C. M = m/2L
D. M = m/L

**5.** tan θ_AV is plotted against which quantity?
A. d
B. 1/d³
C. d/(d² - L²)²
D. (d² - L²)²

## Answers

**1. B** — The needle settles along the resultant of two perpendicular fields, giving B = B_H tan θ. The deck writes it as H = H₀ tan θ; the sine and cosine forms are not the equilibrium condition.

**2. A** — East-west puts the magnet's axial field at right angles to B_H, which is what makes the tangent law apply. North-south is the broadside-on Tan B position, with a different formula.

**3. D** — Eight: both needle ends, both polarities, both sides of the galvanometer. Each pair removes a different error, so they are not just repeats.

**4. B** — M = 2mL, since 2L is the magnet's full length, so pole strength is m = M/2L. Treating L as the full length is the usual slip.

**5. C** — The working equation makes tan θ a straight line through the origin against d/(d² - L²)². Plotting against d gives a useless curve, and 1/d³ is only the far-field limit.
