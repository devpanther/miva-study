# Monday — GST_112 fast-hour check

*Popular culture, the four waves of Nigerian music, and the #EndSARS crisis.*
*Five questions, straight after the fast hour. Recall only. Score out of 5.*

**1.** When was the term 'popular culture' coined?
A. The mid-19th century
B. The early 20th century
C. Just after World War II
D. The 1960s

**2.** #EndSARS began as a hashtag on Twitter in which year?
A. 2015
B. 2017
C. 2019
D. 2020

**3.** On what date did the army open fire at the Lekki toll gate?
A. 8 October 2017
B. 25 February 2023
C. 20 October 2020
D. 11 March 2023

**4.** Fuji emerged as a fusion of Apala and Ajisari in which wave?
A. First Wave, 1950-67
B. Third Wave, 1980-90
C. Fourth Wave, 1992 to date
D. Second Wave, 1967-80

**5.** Which scholar argues the Nigerian Police have not been decolonised?
A. Moses Ochonu
B. Abimbola Oyarinu
C. E. Effetti
D. T. Olasoji

## Answers

**1. A** — The deck says the term was coined in the mid-19th century, in contrast to the official culture of the state. Post-war mass media and the 1960s belong later in the same origin story.

**2. B** — It began as a hashtag in 2017 and metamorphosed into a crisis in October 2020. A question about when the movement gained momentum wants 2020; when it began wants 2017.

**3. C** — The Nigerian Army fired on protesters at Lekki on 20 October 2020, ending a three-week protest. The 2023 dates are the deck's stated election days.

**4. D** — Fuji is second wave, Islamic in influence and lower-class, with Barrister and Kollington. Apala itself is first wave (Haruna Ishola); Waka and Afro Juju are third wave.

**5. A** — Ochonu says colonial policing was about suppression of revolts and that brute force was its primary operating philosophy. Oyarinu ties SARS instead to post-oil-boom economic turmoil.
