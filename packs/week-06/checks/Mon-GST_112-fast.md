# Monday — GST_112 fast-hour check

*The indigenous trade system of 1500 to 1800 AD and Nigeria's economic story from 1960 to the loss of the American market.*
*Five questions, straight after the fast hour. Recall only. Score out of 5.*

**1.** Which river was pivotal to the growth of the indigenous trade system?
A. The Benue
B. The Niger
C. The Cross River
D. The Kaduna

**2.** How were craft and trading skills transmitted in the indigenous system?
A. Mission schools
B. Guild examinations
C. Development corporations
D. The apprenticeship system

**3.** What share of the workforce did agriculture absorb in 1960?
A. Over 25 per cent
B. Over 40 per cent
C. Over 75 per cent
D. Over 90 per cent

**4.** Under which regime were austerity measures adopted after the mid-1980s oil crunch?
A. The Buhari regime
B. The Babangida regime
C. The Abacha regime
D. The Gowon regime

**5.** Which country replaced the United States as the major buyer of Nigeria's crude?
A. China
B. Brazil
C. India
D. Indonesia

## Answers

**1. B** — The lesson names the Niger River as pivotal: it carried bulk goods cheaply and linked the interior to the coast. The others are Nigerian rivers but are not the artery the material names.

**2. D** — A young person served a master for a fixed term and was then established in the trade, reproducing the craft economy without formal schooling. Development corporations belong to the regional politics of the 1950s and 1960s.

**3. C** — Agriculture took over 75 per cent of the workforce in 1960. The over-fifty-per-cent figure is the neighbouring trap: that is agriculture's share of GDP before independence, which is output rather than employment.

**4. A** — Austerity measures came first, under Buhari, as Nigeria's own response to the crunch; the Structural Adjustment Programme followed under IMF pressure. Abacha is remembered for looting the 1990s boom.

**5. C** — The US, the largest buyer, announced reduced purchases in 2014 because of its own shale oil, and India replaced it. China, Brazil and Indonesia appear in the lesson only in the list of countries more populated than Nigeria.
