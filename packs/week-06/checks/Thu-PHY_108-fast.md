# Thursday — PHY_108 fast-hour check

*Charging a capacitor: the time constant, obtaining C from the graph, the circuit wiring, capacitor behaviour and the procedure.*
*Five questions, straight after the fast hour. Recall only. Score out of 5.*

**1.** At one time constant, what percentage of the maximum voltage has the capacitor reached?
A. 36.8 per cent
B. 63.2 per cent
C. 86.5 per cent
D. 99.3 per cent

**2.** With the experimental time constant read off the graph, how is the capacitance found?
A. C = τR
B. C = R/τ
C. C = τ/R
D. C = τ + R

**3.** How is the voltmeter connected in the charging circuit?
A. In series with the resistor
B. In series with the capacitor
C. In series with the switch key
D. In parallel across the capacitor

**4.** What does a capacitor do to alternating and direct current?
A. Passes AC and blocks DC
B. Passes DC and blocks AC
C. Blocks both
D. Passes both equally

**5.** How often is the voltmeter read during the trial?
A. Once at the start and once at the end
B. Every second
C. Every 10 seconds, for at least 10 readings
D. Every 63 seconds

## Answers

**1. B** — Charging reaches 63.2 per cent of maximum in one time constant; 36.8 per cent is what remains while discharging. 86.5 per cent is two time constants and 99.3 per cent is five.

**2. C** — Since τ = RC, dividing the experimental τ by the known R gives C. Remember to put R in ohms, not kilohms, or the answer is out by a factor of a thousand.

**3. D** — The voltmeter must read the potential difference across the capacitor, so it goes in parallel with it, which is why it is made of very high resistance. Resistor, capacitor, switch and supply are the parts in series.

**4. A** — This is why capacitors are used as filters in DC supplies. The reverse statement is the common slip.

**5. C** — Readings are taken every 10 seconds until the voltage reaches its maximum, with at least ten points so the asymptote V0 is well established. That interval works because the time constant here is about 10 seconds, comfortably longer than reaction time.
