# Wednesday — GST_122 fast-hour check

*The ordered steps and conventions for writing a summary, a letter and a report.*
*Five questions, straight after the fast hour. Recall only. Score out of 5.*

**1.** In summary writing, which step follows identifying the thesis?
A. Condense information
B. Maintain structure
C. Highlight key points
D. Check for coherence

**2.** What must a summary be written in?
A. Your own words, with no new material or opinion
B. The original's own sentences, kept intact
C. Direct quotation with commentary added
D. Your own words, with your judgement of the argument

**3.** Which closing goes with a named recipient such as Dear Mr Azeez?
A. Yours faithfully
B. Yours truly
C. Kind regards only
D. Yours sincerely

**4.** Which report section states the methods used to gather the data?
A. Introduction
B. Methodology
C. Findings
D. Conclusions

**5.** What step do summary, letter and report writing all end with?
A. Adding references and appendices
B. A final review and edit
C. Writing a closing salutation
D. Making recommendations

## Answers

**1. C** — The order runs read carefully, identify the thesis, highlight key points, condense, maintain structure, eliminate details, check coherence, review. The other three options are all later steps in that same list.

**2. A** — A summary restates the key points in your own words and carries no new material, no personal opinion and no direct quotation. Stitching the original's own sentences together fails the condensing step however short the result is.

**3. D** — Yours sincerely pairs with a named salutation, and the sample letter closes that way. Yours faithfully belongs with the impersonal Dear Sir or Madam.

**4. B** — Methodology presents the methods used, ensuring transparency and reliability. The introduction gives topic, purpose and scope, findings give the evidence, and conclusions interpret it.

**5. B** — All three end with a review and revise or review and edit for clarity, coherence and accuracy that also checks the piece achieves its purpose. References and recommendations belong only to reports, and the closing salutation only to letters.
