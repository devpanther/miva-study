# Monday — GST_112 fast-hour check

*The four indicators of pre-colonial intergroup relations, with their named origin, conflict, trade and religion examples.*
*Five questions, straight after the fast hour. Recall only. Score out of 5.*

**1.** Which four indicators show the nature of pre-colonial intergroup interaction?
A. Traditions of origin, conflict, trade and commerce, religion
B. Language, culture, trade and commerce, religion
C. Traditions of origin, language, marriage, warfare
D. Conflict, trade and commerce, marriage, kingship

**2.** Which ancestor do the Hausa trace their origin to?
A. Saif
B. Kisra
C. Bayajidda
D. Eweka

**3.** Which two Hausa states fought wars over control of trade routes?
A. Oyo and Nupe
B. Kano and Katsina
C. Bariba and Yoruba
D. Igala and Nsukka Igbo

**4.** The Jukun exchanged salt for which Tiv commodity?
A. Potash
B. Shea butter
C. Brasswork
D. Firewood

**5.** The Yoruba deity Igunu is traced to which Nupe original?
A. Ibini Ukpabi
B. Ekpe
C. Ndakogbaya
D. Bori

## Answers

**1. A** — The four are traditions of origin, conflict, trade and commerce, and religion. Culture and language are the two identifiers of a group, a different list, and marriage and kingship are not indicators at all.

**2. C** — Bayajidda is the Hausa ancestor, traced to Baghdad, who settled among the Kanuri and married the Mai's daughter. Saif is the Kanuri ancestor, Kisra founded the Yoruba and Borgu kingdoms, and Eweka belongs to the Yoruba-Benin link.

**3. B** — Kano and Katsina were the Hausa states that fought over trade routes. Oyo fought Nupe with a Bariba-Yoruba alliance, and the Igala-Nsukka Igbo wars were territorial.

**4. D** — The Jukun gave salt and received firewood from the Tiv. Potash, shea butter and brasswork are on the separate list of Nupe goods traded to the Yoruba.

**5. C** — Igunu is the Yoruba name for the Nupe Ndakogbaya. Ibini Ukpabi is Ibibio in origin, Ekpe is Efik in origin, and Bori is the Hausaland spirit-possession cult.
