# Thursday — PHY_108 fast-hour check

*Ohm's law and its proviso, non-ohmic devices, parallel behaviour, meter placement and the milliamp unit trap.*
*Five questions, straight after the fast hour. Recall only. Score out of 5.*

**1.** Ohm's law holds provided which quantity stays constant?
A. Temperature
B. Current
C. Resistance
D. Potential difference

**2.** Which three devices does the week name as non-ohmic?
A. Ammeter, voltmeter, breadboard
B. Varistor, photoresistor, thermistor
C. Resistor, capacitor, inductor
D. Varistor, rheostat, ammeter

**3.** In a parallel combination, what is the same across every resistor?
A. The current
B. The resistance
C. The potential difference
D. The power dissipated

**4.** How is the ammeter connected in the Ohm's law circuit?
A. In parallel with the resistor
B. Across the DC power supply
C. In parallel with the voltmeter
D. In series with the resistor

**5.** Plotting volts against milliamps gives a gradient in which unit?
A. Ohms
B. Kilohms
C. Milliohms
D. Amperes

## Answers

**1. A** — Current is proportional to potential difference provided temperature and other physical conditions stay constant; that clause earns the mark. Current and voltage are the two quantities being varied, and constant resistance is the consequence, not the condition.

**2. B** — Varistors, photoresistors and thermistors have a resistance that varies with voltage, light or temperature, giving a curved V-I graph. The other options list apparatus or components the slides never call non-ohmic.

**3. C** — Parallel resistors share the same potential difference and split the current; series resistors share the same current and split the voltage.

**4. D** — The ammeter must carry the current it measures, so it goes in series. The voltmeter is the one connected in parallel, straddling the resistor whose potential difference it reads.

**5. B** — Volts per milliamp is kilohms, so a gradient of 1 means 1 kilohm, not 1 ohm. Convert before comparing with the 1 kilohm default value and its 5 per cent tolerance.
