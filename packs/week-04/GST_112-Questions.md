## Multiple choice (12)

**1.** Which constitution first introduced the elective principle in Nigeria?
A) Richards, 1947  B) Clifford, 1922  C) Macpherson, 1951  D) Lyttleton, 1954

**2.** The Richards Constitution (1947) provided a bicameral legislature in:
A) All three regions  B) The North only  C) The West only  D) Lagos only

**3.** Under the Macpherson Constitution, the renamed central legislative body — the House of Representatives — had how many members?
A) 45  B) 90  C) 136  D) 12

**4.** The Lyttleton Constitution of 1954 was notable for being introduced by:
A) A Governor-General  B) A Colonial Secretary, not a governor  C) The Prime Minister  D) A Regional Premier

**5.** Which governor abolished the office of the Lieutenant Governor and the provincial court, and introduced the doctrine of separation of powers?
A) Hugh Clifford  B) Donald Cameron  C) Bernard Bourdillon  D) Graham Thompson

**6.** The Nigerian Council, set up by Lord Lugard in 1914, performed:
A) Legislative functions with veto power  B) Only advisory functions  C) Judicial review functions  D) Executive appointment functions

**7.** The Nigerian National Democratic Party (NNDP), founded by Herbert Macaulay in 1923, published which newspaper?
A) West African Pilot  B) Daily Service  C) Lagos Daily News  D) Nigerian Tribune

**8.** The Nigeria Youth Movement (NYM) won all elective seats from the NNDP in:
A) 1935  B) 1938  C) 1944  D) 1951

**9.** Who was the NCNC's first General Secretary, later becoming President after Herbert Macaulay's death?
A) Obafemi Awolowo  B) Nnamdi Azikiwe  C) Ernest Ikoli  D) Ahmadu Bello

**10.** The Action Group grew out of a cultural organisation founded in 1945 by Awolowo, the Ooni, and Ajasin, called:
A) Jamiyyar Mutanen Arewa  B) Egbe Omo Oduduwa  C) Lagos Youth Movement  D) NCNC

**11.** The Northern Peoples Congress (NPC), established in 1951, emerged from the cultural organisation:
A) Egbe Omo Oduduwa  B) Jamiyyar Mutanen Arewa  C) Lagos Youth Movement  D) West African Pilot Society

**12.** According to the source, the Macpherson Constitution collapsed inside three years because of:
A) The Berlin Conference  B) Enahoro's motion for independence  C) The Kirk Commission of Enquiry  D) The Richards Constitution's failure

## Short answer (3)

**13.** Name the four constitutions in chronological order with their years, and state one thing each introduced.

**14.** Trace the leadership chain of the NCNC from its founding in August 1944 to Azikiwe's presidency, naming both men and their original roles.

**15.** List the four named founders of the Northern Peoples Congress (NPC), and name the cultural organisation it grew out of.

## Answers

**1. B** — the **Clifford Constitution (1922)** introduced the **elective principle**, which in turn triggered political parties and newspapers.
**2. B** — Richards (1947) gave a **bicameral** legislature to the **North only**; the other regions and the centre stayed unicameral.
**3. C** — Macpherson's central House of Representatives, the renamed Legislative Council, had **136 members** and was made supreme.
**4. B** — Lyttleton was a **Colonial Secretary**, not a governor like his predecessors (Clifford, Richards, Macpherson all governed in post).
**5. B** — **Donald Cameron** (Governor, 1930–1936) abolished the office of Lieutenant Governor and the provincial court, and introduced separation of powers. (Bourdillon, who followed him from 1935, instead prepared the ground for the next constitution.)
**6. B** — the Nigerian Council (1914) performed **advisory functions only**, though it laid the foundation for later constitutional development.
**7. C** — the NNDP published the **Lagos Daily News**. (West African Pilot belongs to NCNC; Daily Service belongs to NYM.)
**8. B** — NYM won all elective seats from the NNDP in **1938**; it was later destroyed by internal disputes in 1944.
**9. B** — **Nnamdi Azikiwe** was NCNC's first General Secretary and became President after Macaulay died. Macaulay himself was the party's first President.
**10. B** — **Egbe Omo Oduduwa** (1945), founded by Awolowo, the Ooni, and Ajasin, is what the Action Group (formed in Ibadan, 1951) grew out of.
**11. B** — the NPC (1951) emerged from **Jamiyyar Mutanen Arewa**, a Northern cultural organisation. (Egbe Omo Oduduwa is the Action Group's precursor, not the NPC's.)
**12. B** — the Macpherson Constitution did not survive three years because of **Enahoro's motion for independence**. (The Kirk Commission and Berlin Conference belong to earlier weeks' material, not this constitution.)
**13.** **Clifford (1922)** — first written constitution, introduced the elective principle. **Richards (1947)** — introduced regionalism (3 regions) with a bicameral legislature in the North only. **Macpherson (1951)** — created the 136-member House of Representatives and the Council of Ministers. **Lyttleton (1954)** — introduced the federal system, renamed Lieutenant Governors as Governors, and created the office of Premier.
**14.** The NCNC was founded in **August 1944**. **Herbert Macaulay** was its **first President**; **Nnamdi Azikiwe** was its **first General Secretary**. When Macaulay died, Azikiwe succeeded him and **became President**.
**15.** The NPC's named founders: **Ahmadu Bello (Sardauna of Sokoto, President), Tafawa Balewa, Yahaya Gusau, and Aminu Kano**. It grew out of the cultural organisation **Jamiyyar Mutanen Arewa**.
