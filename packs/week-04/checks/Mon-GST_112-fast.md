# Monday — GST_112 fast-hour check

*The four constitutions, the early political parties, and the 1914 amalgamation debate.*
*Five questions, straight after the fast hour. Recall only. Score out of 5.*

**1.** Which constitution introduced the elective principle to Nigeria?
A. Clifford, 1922
B. Richards, 1947
C. Macpherson, 1951
D. Lyttleton, 1954

**2.** Who introduced the federal system in the 1954 constitution?
A. Sir Arthur Richards
B. Sir John Macpherson
C. Sir Oliver Lyttleton
D. Sir Hugh Clifford

**3.** The West African Pilot was the newspaper of which party?
A. NNDP
B. NYM
C. NPC
D. NCNC

**4.** What ended the Macpherson Constitution within three years?
A. The 1954 federal reforms
B. Enahoro's motion for independence
C. The NCNC protest delegation
D. Abolishing the Lieutenant Governor's office

**5.** One school of thought holds that the 1914 amalgamation merely formalised what?
A. Pre-existing inter-group relations
B. The elective principle
C. Northern regional autonomy
D. The federal system

## Answers

**1. A** — Clifford's 1922 constitution, the first written one, introduced the elective principle, which led to political parties and newspapers. Richards brought regionalism and Lyttleton federalism.

**2. C** — Lyttleton introduced federalism, regional Premiers and Lagos as Federal Capital Territory, and was a Colonial Secretary, not a Governor like the other three.

**3. D** — West African Pilot belongs to the NCNC. The NNDP had the Lagos Daily News and the NYM the Daily Service; the NPC is not paired with a newspaper here.

**4. B** — Enahoro's motion for independence brought it down inside three years. The NCNC delegation protested the 1946 constitution, and Cameron abolished the Lieutenant Governor's office in the 1930s.

**5. A** — One school says it only formalised inter-group relations already in place; the other says it created a deeply divided nation. The elective principle, autonomy and federalism belong to the constitutions instead.
