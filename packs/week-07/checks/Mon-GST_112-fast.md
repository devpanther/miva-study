# Monday — GST_112 fast-hour check

*Where Nigerian human rights sit in law, and the five common abuses with their figures and statute.*
*Five questions, straight after the fast hour. Recall only. Score out of 5.*

**1.** Nigeria's fundamental human rights are contained in which part of the 1999 Constitution?
A. Chapter II, Sections 13-24
B. Chapter Three, Sections 25-32
C. Chapter IV, Sections 33-46
D. The Seventh Schedule

**2.** Which instrument does the lesson credit with making these rights enforceable in Nigerian courts?
A. Article 1 of the UN Declaration of Human Rights
B. The African Charter on Human and People's Rights
C. The 2009 Rules
D. The Violence Against Persons (Prohibition) Act

**3.** How did the National Human Rights Commission's recorded complaints change from 2020 to 2021?
A. Rose from 1,287,280 to 1,701,519
B. Fell from 1,701,519 to 1,287,280
C. Rose from 367,000 to 1,287,280
D. Held steady at about 1,701,519

**4.** The #EndSARS protests of October 2020 are attached to which of the five named abuses?
A. Rape
B. Child marriage
C. Extra-judicial killing
D. Domestic violence

**5.** The Violence Against Persons (Prohibition) Act was signed into law in which year?
A. 1979
B. 1999
C. 2009
D. 2015

## Answers

**1. C** — Chapter IV, Sections 33 to 46 is where the lesson places the fundamental human rights. Chapter II holds the Fundamental Objectives and Directive Principles, Chapter Three deals with citizenship, and the Seventh Schedule holds the Oath of Allegiance.

**2. B** — The lesson names the African Charter on Human and People's Rights as the instrument guaranteeing that the rights are enforceable in Nigerian courts of law. The UDHR supplies only the definition and the Article 1 quotation, the 2009 Rules are the enforcement rules said to have shortcomings, and the VAPP Act is about domestic violence.

**3. A** — The NHRC received 1,287,280 complaints in 2020 and 1,701,519 cases in 2021, so the figure rose. Option two reverses the direction, and 367,000 is the separate 2021 count of women and gender-related issues, not an NHRC total.

**4. C** — The lesson cites #EndSARS as what brought international attention to police brutality, under extra-judicial killing by the security forces. The other three are the neighbouring abuses on the same list, each carrying its own evidence: 367,000 cases for rape, the 43 percent figure for child marriage, and the VAPP Act for domestic violence.

**5. D** — The VAPP Act of 2015 criminalises forms of abuse and provides for reporting, prosecution and survivor support. 1979 is the constitution that introduced the Fundamental Objectives and the Fundamental Human Rights, 1999 is the constitution that now contains the rights, and 2009 is the year of the Rules said to have shortcomings.
