In this enlightening lesson on concord, we unraveled the intricacies of grammatical agreement, emphasizing
its pivotal role in effective communication. Concord, a set of rules governing the agreement between different
elements in a sentence, ensures linguistic harmony. The lesson delved into subject-verb agreement as a
fundamental rule and explored the nuanced “Rule of Proximity Agreement,” shedding light on its complexity
and common misunderstandings. The presence of “or” in a sentence can influence the choice between
singular and plural verbs, emphasizing the importance of understanding sentence structure. Concord rules,
though seemingly complex, contribute significantly to clear and effective communication, fostering credibility
in written and spoken language.



    Bullet point summary

     distractions caused by bad grammar.
•    Subject-verb agreement dictates that the number of the subject must match the number of the verb
     in a sentence.
•    The “Rule of Proximity Agreement” adds complexity, requiring the verb or noun to align with the closest
     subject in sentences with multiple elements.
•    The presence of “or” in a sentence can influence the choice between singular and plural verbs, emphasizing
     the importance of understanding sentence structure.
